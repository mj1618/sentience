## Owner's hypothesis: consciousness = sentience + thought

Status: **viable, needs two amendments.**
1. Split "motivator" into teacher (felt valence) and driver (wanting), per C9.
2. Account for neutral experience (seeing grey) and for wakefulness without affect or
   thought (auto-activation patients' "empty mind"). Either these are faint valence, or
   there is a third ingredient.

## Next scenarios

1. **What is missing from a reward signal?** Thermostats and RL agents have evaluative
   signals. Candidate extra ingredients: a body with something at stake (homeostasis),
   one unified scale (C6), the firewall (C7), a self-model. Build agents with each subset
   and look for behavioural signatures that only the full set shows.
2. **Teacher or driver?** Lesion each in simulation and match the pattern to asymbolia,
   auto-activation deficit, and wanting/liking dissociations.
3. **Where is the line in the animal kingdom?** Use C5 (lifetime novelty) to predict
   which lineages should have valence and compare with trade-off experiments.
4. **Cheapest decisive physics experiment.** Design a replication of the xenon-isotope
   anaesthesia result with enough power to settle it.
5. **Neutral experience.** Is there any experience with zero valence, or is "plain
   seeing" valence at low intensity? What would distinguish these?
