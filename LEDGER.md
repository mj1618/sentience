## Owner's hypothesis: consciousness = sentience + thought

Viable; not yet distinguished from Panksepp/Damasio/Solms/Dickinson. Open issues:
wanting vs liking (teacher or driver); whether neutral awareness is a third ingredient,
the arousal axis of feeling, or a zero point of valence; whether unfelt valuation exists.

## Conjecture queue (round 3)

From the red team and our own list. Each has a test that could fail.

1. **Not one thing.** "Sentience" is a bundle. Test: under deepening anaesthesia or brain injury, do pain, hunger, itch and pleasure go at one threshold or piecemeal?
2. **Report threshold only.** One valuation system; "felt" is what clears a self-monitoring threshold. Test: signal-detection reanalysis of the unfelt-valence studies.
3. **Interrupt.** Feeling is how urgent matters pre-empt a one-thing-at-a-time planner; intensity tracks urgency to switch, not value. Test: simulation plus dual-task literature.
4. **One feeler per bottleneck.** Test: does split-brain surgery double hedonic conflict? Octopus arms?
5. **Organisation-sourced or role-conditioned physics.** A field sourced by organisation, or a chooser that keeps every single-event frequency exact. Test: what would a detector beside an awake brain need to see; what statistic would expose role-conditioning?
6. **Compression residue.** The felt quality is what a self-model cannot represent of its own valuation. Test: simulation with an explicit read channel of varying capacity.
7. **Bridge across time.** Feeling holds an event "live" until its consequences arrive. Test: does learning across a gap need awareness (trace conditioning)?

## Process rules (added round 2)

- Same generosity for every hypothesis within a comparison.
- Every sim gets a stronger opponent and a parameter sweep before it is cited.
- Option lists carry a "not yet conceived" row.
- Literature claims tagged; snippet-only claims never carry a conclusion alone.
