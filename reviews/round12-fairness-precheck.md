# Round 12 — fairness pre-check of Scenario 21, Part 1

Made before evidence gathering; no research/21-* file was read. Sources: Europe PMC abstracts of the papers cited; claims from general knowledge are flagged "(GK)".

## 1. N is a catch-all, and T1/T3/T5 are not its rivals

"Organisation" in N is unbounded. Functionalists (workspace, recurrent processing, predictive processing) hold that temporal organisation — sustained recurrence, integration windows, anticipation — *is* part of the organisation. So a positive result on rows 1, 2, 5, 6 or 9 can be absorbed by N, and the "What would count" clause can never be met.

**Redraw the line.** Two different questions are mixed:

- **Q-org (which organisation?).** Is a given temporal *organisation* necessary for feeling? Either answer is compatible with N, so each such claim needs its own rival.
- **Q-time (time itself?).** Does a property of *physical time* matter beyond organisation?

**N\*, fixed now:** feeling depends only on causal organisation including order and *relative* timing. It is unchanged if the whole system (and its inputs) is uniformly slowed or sped up, or implemented with different energy dissipation. N\* fails only if absolute duration, absolute rate, thermodynamic irreversibility of the substrate, or objective passage makes a difference with organisation held fixed.

## 2. Each conjecture against its proponents

- **T1.** "However strong" is not Libet's claim (GK): his ~500 ms was at near-threshold intensity, with stronger trains needing less time, and a single skin pulse is felt. Workspace and recurrent-processing accounts require sustained *neural* activity, which a brief stimulus can trigger (Q-org). IIT's grain is where cause–effect power peaks, and Husserl/Varela's specious present is a structure (retention–now–protention); neither is a duration floor.
- **T2.** Proponents claim a *signature*: irreversibility "covaries with the level of conscious awareness" (de la Fuente 2023). Stikvoort 2025 derives it from asymmetric effective connectivity, i.e. from organisation. None claims it constitutes experience, is sufficient, or scales with feeling. "The more irreversible, the more conscious" over-states them; Lynn 2021 shows it rises with physical and cognitive effort. Row 4 has no proponent.
- **T3.** Friston 2018: mind arises when self-evidencing has "temporal thickness or counterfactual depth". This is a sub-personal generative model, not episodic imagining of one's future. Solms (GK) places affect in brainstem homeostatic error and would predict intact feeling in hippocampal amnesia. Row 5 tests a claim nobody makes.
- **T4.** Kent & Wittmann claim only that theories neglect extended, flowing time. Husserl's time-structure is pre-reflective, so a *report* of timelessness does not remove it; Northoff's theory concerns the brain's own temporal dynamics, not felt passage. Craig 2009 runs the other way: feeling states build subjective time. No one named predicts row 7's conjecture cell.
- **T5.** Herzog: discrete percepts follow unconscious processing of variable length (hundreds of ms) — no fixed rate, no physical rhythm. VanRullen 2016: "not one but several rhythms", task- and modality-dependent. Only Orch-OR ties moments to a physical event, with variable interval (τ ≈ ħ/E_G) (GK). Discreteness is Q-org.
- **T6.** No mainstream holder. 40 Hz was a binding hypothesis, later dropped by its authors (GK). Orch-OR comes nearest.

**Missing.** (a) **T7, objective becoming:** feeling requires real passage (presentism, Bergson, Whitehead, Smolin's temporal naturalism). Philosophical; no existing evidence bears on it. Mark it unscored. (b) **Retention** need not be separate: it is the Husserlian core of T1/T4. Fold it in as T4\* (below), distinct from long-term memory.

## 4. Amendment (ready to append)

**Conjectures.** T1\* a feeling requires neural activity sustained beyond a floor that intensity cannot buy off. T2\* (marker) irreversibility of large-scale brain dynamics tracks presence of experience across states; T2c (constituent; authors' own) it tracks experience when activity level is controlled, and scales with felt intensity. T3\* feeling requires a system that predicts its own future bodily states; T3e (authors' own) it requires episodic future thinking. T4\* feeling requires retention across a window (strong form: requires felt passage). T5\* experience updates in discrete steps. T6\* feeling changes or is lost under uniform rate change. T7 unscored.

| # | Test, with criterion fixed now | Conjecture fails if | Rival fails if |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Detection/felt-intensity reports vs duration and intensity of *cortical* activity (direct stimulation, masking, TMS) | Reciprocity holds down to the shortest durations tested: more intensity always compensates | A floor exists (≥2 independent paradigms) where no tested intensity yields report |
| 3 | Irreversibility in (a) REM dreaming and ketamine at doses with later-reported experience; (b) generalised seizures, and unresponsive states with normal metabolic/activity level; arousal and signal power controlled | Low in (a) or high in (b) | High in (a) and low in (b) across ≥2 datasets — refutes "mere arousal marker", not N\* |
| 4 | Self-rated valence intensity vs irreversibility, activity-matched | No relation, or equal rise with neutral effort | Relation survives matching |
| 5 | Self-rated valence and autonomic response in hippocampal amnesia: tests T3e only | Ratings in normal range | Ratings flat |
| 6 | T3\*: feeling with interoceptive/homeostatic prediction disrupted (pure autonomic failure, high spinal injury, insular lesions) | Self-rated valence normal | Reduced in step with deficit |
| 7 | Reports of timelessness with concurrent or immediate self-rated valence | Valence rated intense — refutes strong T4 only | Valence uniformly flat |
| 9 | Periodicity in perception | No single rate across tasks and modalities | One rate, tied to one rhythm — Q-org only |
| 10 | Self-rated feeling under mild hypothermia, fever, rate-altering drugs, where cognition is intact | Feeling reported unchanged in kind across the range | Feeling lost while task performance and relative timing are preserved |

**Scoring rules.**
1. Rows 1, 5, 6, 9 are scored against their own rival, and never count for or against N\*.
2. Only rows 3–4 (T2c) and 10 can count against N\*, and only with organisation or activity matched.
3. Rows 2 and 8 are dropped as discriminators.
4. Feeling means self-rated valence; level-of-consciousness measures count for rows 1, 3, 9 only.
5. A row 3 result is reported as marker, not cause; states are classed (a)/(b) blind to the result.
6. Loss of feeling under cooling or anaesthesia is uninformative for row 10.

**Cannot be decided by evidence:** T7; T4\* in its implicit (Husserlian) form; and N\* versus T6\* for rate changes beyond what a living brain tolerates.
