# Round 10 — fairness pre-check of Scenario 19, Part 1

Made before scoring; no research/19-* file read. Sources: LeDoux & Brown 2017, Rolls et al. 2020 (Europe PMC abstracts); otherwise general knowledge, unverified.

## 1. K\* is again not stated as its proponents hold it

- **The K cells are the round 9 strawman K.** "Preserved wherever the danger is known" and "intact if value knowledge is intact" treat the value representation as declarative knowledge. K\* means the orbitofrontal/ventromedial/cingulate value signal used by the planner. Round 9's amended table already gave K\* for asymbolia as "Lost, because the aversive value signal is lost"; row 1 contradicts it.
- **On K\*, rows 1–4 damage the value signal itself.** Insular/cingulate lesions, opioids and serotonergic blunting act on the circuits K\* says carry value. K\* predicts impairment, as H does. In row 2 the teaching signal never existed; K\* predicts poor protection improving through instructed and social value, as H does.
- **Separable in principle? It depends on which K\*.**
  - **K\*-HO** (LeDoux & Brown; Rolls): feeling is a further cortical, higher-order construction on "nonconscious inputs" from value and survival circuits. Feeling and value signal are distinct states. Separable.
  - **K\*-ID** (first-order or workspace reading): the feeling just is the value representation being accessible to the planner. Then **H and K\*-ID are empirically equivalent**. Model-based RL is silent between the two.

## 2. H can fail, but the requirements are neither sound nor symmetric

- **Asymmetry.** To refute H only requirements 1 and 3 are needed (no feeling, flexible action intact). Requirement 2 matters only for refuting K\*.
- **Requirement 2 is not a fair test.** K\* separates "cold" semantic knowledge from the value signal that drives choice; ventromedial patients are its standard example of the first without the second. Stating or ranking shows only the first.
- **Circularity.** For K\*, devaluation-sensitive choice is itself the assay of intact model-based value. Requirement 2 needs a measure independent of requirement 3 and of the feeling report.
- **Bodily measures are on the wrong side.** LeDoux treats autonomic and defensive responses as outputs of the survival circuit, not as feeling. For K\* their absence shows a damaged value signal.
- **H's escapes.** "Other felt consequences" (row 2); unreported feeling; reclassifying a laboratory choice as "not self-initiated".

## 4. Amendment (append before scoring)

**Definitions.** K\* is split into K\*-HO and K\*-ID. H versus K\*-ID is declared **not empirically separable**; no row is scored between them.

**Requirements.**

1. **Feeling absent**: self-rated felt valence toward the outcome, anticipatory and consummatory (including craving, dread, fear of social consequences, anticipated regret), at floor. Self-report is the criterion for both sides; H may not appeal to unreported feeling.
2. **Value signal intact** (needed only to count against K\*), shown by at least two of: (a) orbitofrontal/ventromedial activity scaling with outcome value and updating after devaluation; (b) intact anticipatory autonomic or Pavlovian responses, cue-triggered approach or transfer; (c) coherent, transitive, incentive-compatible valuations when externally prompted (bids, forced choice). Verbal statement or ranking alone does not qualify.
3. **Flexible action** by the round 9 criteria 1–4. Prompted criterion-meeting choice counts; H may not reclassify it as not self-initiated. Real-life behaviour is secondary evidence.

**Scoring.**
- 1 + 3 intact: **H fails** (2 not needed).
- 1 + 2 + 3 lost, with prompted performance also lost or an initiation deficit excluded: **K\*-HO fails**.
- 1 met, 2 not shown, 3 lost: **no test**.

## 5. If nothing separates them

Against K\*-ID no observation can. Against K\*-HO, separation exists only if reported feeling and the value signal ever come apart; if they never do, that favours identity, not H. The choice then rests on argument: which theory of consciousness is right (first-order versus higher-order, i.e. whether feeling is a separate state at all); whether "the feeling does the work" adds anything to "the representation does the work" (parsimony; causal exclusion); and whether H's "necessary" is empirical or a definition of feeling as accessible value. H should then be stated as an identity claim, not a rival causal hypothesis.
