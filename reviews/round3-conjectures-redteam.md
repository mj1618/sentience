## Further conjectures (not in the queue)

1. **Valence is a rate, not a level.** Felt good/bad tracks the rate of change of a regulated error, not its size. *Test:* clamp the deficit (hypertonic saline, CO₂, heat), vary only its trajectory. Fails if ratings follow level regardless of direction.
2. **Ownership gate.** Valence is felt only for signals bound to a self-model. *Test:* at fixed nociceptive input, manipulate ownership (rubber-hand illusion, asomatognosia, clinical depersonalisation). Fails if unpleasantness, not just localisation, is unchanged when ownership is abolished.
3. **Slow-medium conjecture.** Feeling is the state of slow, diffuse chemical signalling (peptides, opioids), not fast synaptic traffic. *Test:* psychophysics of valence reversal. Fails if felt valence can flip cleanly within ~100 ms, or if peptide-receptor blockade leaves felt valence intact while behaviour changes.
4. **No-policy signal.** Feeling marks situations with no automatised response; it fades as a policy is learned. *Test:* overtrain an escape response to a fixed noxious stimulus. Fails if unpleasantness at matched input does not decline with automaticity.
5. **Honest-signal origin.** Feeling evolved to make need displays credible to kin. *Test:* comparative; solitary species with no parental care should lack the markers. Likely to fail on octopus (Crook 2021).
