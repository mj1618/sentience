## Problems

1. **Headline:** "and then turned out to mean something different from what we expected". The reading is post hoc and untested. Replace: "and later checks suggest it may mean something different from what we expected".

2. **"Study 6's headline number was reproduced twice by independent reviewers with their own code, starting from the raw recordings."** Overstated. The first reviewer reproduced the three-dataset interim figure from raw files; the second recomputed the four-dataset figure from the saved per-awakening rows and re-derived only the fourth dataset from raw. Replace: "The three-dataset interim number was reproduced by an independent reviewer from the raw recordings. The final four-dataset number was recomputed by a second reviewer from our per-awakening values, with the fourth dataset re-derived from raw recordings."

3. **"With the fourth dataset added, that particular dependence went away (p 0.038 the other way), but it shows how close to the line this is."** "The other way" reads as a reversed effect; and the first review's other waking-location checks (muscle threshold 2x: p 0.077; 5x: p 0.058) were never redone on four datasets. Replace: "With the fourth dataset added, locating waking the alternative way in those two datasets still passes (effect −0.26, p 0.038). Other reasonable settings of the waking rule in the largest dataset, which gave p 0.06 to 0.08 on three datasets, were not rechecked. Leaving out single participants pushed p above 0.05 in 4 or 5 of 34 cases."

4. **"a weak, whole-brain difference: a sleeper whose brain activity is quieter overall, at every speed, half a minute before being woken is a little more likely to say they were experiencing something."** Stated as a finding; it is post hoc. "Whole-brain" should be whole-scalp. Omits that in Study 5's datasets fast activity leaned the opposite way (+0.18, +0.16, not significant). "Quieter" invites misreading. Replace: "a weak difference across the whole scalp: in these four datasets, recordings had slightly lower power at every speed half a minute before awakenings at which people reported an experience. This was found after the fact and is untested; in Study 5's datasets fast activity leaned, non-significantly, the other way."

5. **"That is a description of sleep depth more than of experience"** and **"better than 'how deeply asleep'"**. Sleep depth was never measured; comparisons were within the same sleep stage; the reviewer says only "fits ... better". Replace: "That may be a difference in how deep sleep is rather than a marker of experience, but we have not measured depth directly" and "we have not found a slow-wave or location-specific signal of experience; the one difference we see may simply be lighter sleep."

6. **"The three datasets from Study 5 have not been used for that measure in that window"**. Misleading: those datasets produced the lead, with slow-wave power in that same window (−0.29), which closely tracks overall power. Replace: "The Study 5 datasets have not had overall power computed in that window, but they are where the slow-wave lead came from, so they are not fully fresh."

7. **"four datasets that had played no part in finding the lead"**. Omits scenario 28's disclosure: in the largest dataset (79% of the weight) an earlier reviewer had already seen an earlier window move an eleven-site estimate (−0.07 to −0.15, p 0.09). Add to "How sure": "Before the plan, a reviewer had seen a hint in the largest dataset that an earlier window gave a larger difference."

8. **"A well-known 2017 study reported that when slow waves are weaker over a 'hot zone' ... sleepers woken moments later report having been experiencing something."** Study 5 review item 13: characterisation unverified ("Cite the paper directly"). Add: "(our summary; we have not re-checked the paper's wording)" and link the paper.

9. **"larger 34 to 14 seconds before waking than in the last 20 seconds"**. Early-versus-late is forbidden as a finding. Add: "We make no claim that the earlier window is better; that comparison was not a test."

10. **"roughly 500 to 600 awakenings per test"**. Totals, not informative awakenings. Append: "(only about 300 of which can contribute)".

11. **"Two independent reviewers then checked ... These checks were made after the result"**. The first reviewer's checks preceded the registered result (three datasets). Replace "after the result" with "after seeing results".

12. **Jargon undefined:** dataset, awakening, "95% interval", "within-person standard deviations", "correction for multiple testing", "high-density", "posterior", "committed", "checksum", "registered". Add to "Words used"; replace "until its checksum verified" with "until the download was verified complete".
