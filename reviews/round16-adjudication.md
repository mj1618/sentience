**(a) Prediction 1: not established.** Unseen cues: masked instrumental learning vanished under a stricter awareness test in a registered replication (Skora 2023, ABS); the fear-conditioning meta-analysis found "no convincing evidence" (Mertens 2020, ABS); other positives rest on post-hoc "unaware" selection or lack replication. Unchanged reported feeling: Korb 2020 is the best support. All accounts predicted "yes"; A loses most.

**(b) Feeling intact, action not following.** Only Howard 2020 is full-text and meets the planning definition (TMS; first block only). Reber 2017 (n=6): not in extinction. Morris 2015: abstract-only. Korb and the schizophrenia studies: not planning tasks.
- *Feeling as THE motivator:* strained — normal feeling was not sufficient for action. Necessity untouched.
- *I.4:* lean contradicted — feeling updated while choice did not use the value; I cannot define felt value as planner-accessible by fiat.
- *X.4:* untested by these feel-but-don't-act cases. Its own dissociation has thin support: SM (fear rated zero, danger stated, approach; n=1), auto-activation deficit (clinical description), Camille 2004 (ABS). None is a planning task.
- *N:* neither contradicted nor confirmed.
- *Converse (feeling reduced, planning intact):* no clean case. De Martino: not planning, feeling unrated. SM: not planning, favours X. Gillan (n=1,413), Donegan, Culbreth, Liang (preprint): planning tasks, but trait or clinician scales, not feeling about the options.

**(c) Weights.** Winkielman 2005: unreplicated, small, unpowered feeling null — suggestive at most. Lamb 1991: n=5, abstract; cannot show absent feeling. Korb 2020: strongest (FT, n=131), but a small food-only effect and a 2:1 Bayesian null. Ferrer 2020 (ABS, d=.29): induced feeling changes behaviour, so feeling is not inert. Placebo studies (ABS): illustrative only.

**(d) Overall.** I: weakened on 4. X: split, as above. N: best on 3, unproven on 2 and 4. A: compatible, thin. The evidence leans modestly toward "feeling is a readout beside the route to action, with a separate breakable link", resting on one true planning study plus weaker ones. The owner's hypothesis must give up sufficiency; it may retain necessity (untested) and causal influence (Ferrer).

**(e) Missing study.** Preregistered, n≥100 per arm. Food/odour devaluation by satiety, choice tested in extinction on untrained cue pairs, plus a two-step task. Trial-wise graded pleasantness and desire for every outcome. Reduced-feeling arm: naltrexone versus placebo. Explicit contingency-knowledge test; working memory matched; facial EMG and effort recorded; equivalence bounds.
- I: knowledge and ratings fall together; planning falls in proportion.
- X: knowledge intact, ratings flat, choice fails to shift.
- N: knowledge intact, ratings flat, choice shifts normally.
- A: choice tracks EMG/effort, not ratings.

## PERMITTED

1. "It is not yet established that value a person does not feel can drive even simple behaviour."
2. "The best-known finding of learning from invisible rewards did not hold up under a stricter awareness test."
3. "In a few studies people reported wanting a food less, yet kept choosing it."
4. "In those studies the feeling was normal and the action did not follow it."
5. "This suggests that feeling by itself is not enough to produce action."
6. "It does not show that feeling is unnecessary."
7. "One patient who reports no fear says snakes are dangerous yet approaches them; this is a single case."
8. "No study has yet tested planning in people whose feeling about the options is measurably reduced."
9. "The evidence leans modestly toward feeling being a readout of value, with a separate link from value to action."

## FORBIDDEN

- "Feeling is not needed for planning."
- "People with little feeling plan normally."
- "Feeling is just a by-product."
- "Unconscious value drives behaviour." / "Unconscious learning has been disproved."
- "Feeling and value never come apart."
- "Science shows feeling is (or is not) the motivator."
- "Amygdala patients show planning without feeling."
- "The identity account has been refuted."
