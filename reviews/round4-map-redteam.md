# Round 4 red team — necessity map (Scenario 8) and machines table (Scenario 10)

Reviewer: adversarial, 2026-10-03. Tags: **[V-full]** full text fetched this review; **[V-abs]** abstract fetched; **[R]** recalled, unchecked. WebSearch unavailable; Europe PMC / PMC only. Full texts came through a summarising fetcher; re-check quotes.

**Headline.** The two full-text cases are reported accurately in outline, but "favours deep-structures-first" does not follow. The map shows only that *limbic and paralimbic cortex is not necessary for reported feeling*. The "generator" test is applied to the hypothalamus more leniently than to the amygdala and insula.

## Item 9 — credence shifts justified

- **Insula/ACC/amygdala-centred accounts of feeling** (Craig-style): down substantially. This is the map's one solid result, on two patients.
- **Cortex-first (higher-order/workspace, lateral PFC + parietal) vs subcortex-first:** essentially **no shift**; at most a few points toward subcortex-first, from hydranencephaly behaviour and the intensity of PAG/hypothalamic stimulation effects, both of which the rival predicts. Likelihood ratio near 1.
- **Distributed/redundant** should gain as much as subcortex-first; it is the reading Roger's own authors give.
- **Function-is-enough vs substrate:** **zero**. Lesion and stimulation data concern where in a brain, not what kind of thing; both families predict every row.
- **Language models:** no shift.
