## Scenario 11 — adjudicator's result, accepted as written

- No row confirmed or contradicted any account.
- Deep-first vs cortex-makes-it-felt: **slight** lean to deep-first (about 1.5:1) against
  the cortex view *as we worded it*; about 1.1:1 against the view as its own proponents
  state it. We wrote the cortex view unfairly: its proponents locate the network in the
  outer (lateral) frontal cortex, and none of them predicts weak emotion in dreams or
  that cutting the cingulate abolishes unpleasantness.
- Distributed-redundant could not have failed on any row. That is a defect of that
  account, not support for it.
- **The decisive rows were empty.** No patient with near-total loss of lateral frontal
  cortex on both sides has had feelings recorded. No deep-stimulation study has asked
  about feeling after every trial. No study has timed the onset of a feeling against
  deep and cortical activity.
- Most informative single finding (adjudicator checked the full text): patient "Roger",
  with insula, anterior cingulate, amygdala and medial frontal cortex destroyed on both
  sides, rated cold-water pain 10 out of 10 in real time on three of four trials, with
  matching face, voice, withdrawal and bodily responses. No memory confound. One patient.

## Scenario 12 — mostly withdrawn

| Claim | Outcome |
|---|---|
| "Any additive source quantity: eliminated" | Withdrawn. Four hand-picked quantities, one of them (signalling events) defined so that only brains score. Entitled only to: *mass, heat output, stored electric energy and a loosely defined event rate do not distinguish a brain from a liver, a kettle or a chip.* |
| "The Sun swamps your own brain" | Withdrawn. True only for a static potential. For field strength the Sun's term is about twice the brain's, and a steady background does not drown a local changing signal. |
| "A pattern cannot source a local field" | Withdrawn; wrong physics. Magnetisation is a pattern and sources a field. |
| Emergent "phase transition" field as live and testable | Downgraded to **a metaphor with no prediction a rival would not also make.** Our signature table mixed two incompatible kinds of transition; hysteresis is solid in mice and flies but contested in humans; one signature is contradicted (the waking brain, not the edge of unconsciousness, is where responsiveness peaks); and all endpoints are responsiveness, not feeling. |
| Phone and liver rows | Wrong numbers; corrected in the reviewer's file. |

**One lead from the review.** For slow electric fields inside tissue, the brain *is* the
dominant source at its own location, by at least ten thousand times over the heart and
mains electricity. That is the one ordinary field for which "brains produce a lot of it,
right where it matters" is true, and we have never assessed the electromagnetic-field
theories on their own terms (Update 2 withdrew our unfair objections but put nothing in
their place). Queued for round 6.
