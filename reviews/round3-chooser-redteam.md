## Recomputations [C]

- 5σ, one-sample, p=½: 10⁷ → 7.9×10⁻⁴; 10¹⁰ → 2.5×10⁻⁵; 10⁻⁸ needs 6.25×10¹⁶. Two-arm: 1.6×10⁻³ and 5×10⁻⁵.
- Xenon, repo's own (wrong) inputs: single pair 29/6.4 = 4.5 → n = 10.2; pooled 31/5.4 = 5.8 → n = 6.3. Neither gives 13; scenario and brief disagree (13 vs 11).
- Xenon, measured inputs: implied isoflurane ED50 ≈ 0.64%, extrapolation factor 4.5–4.7. Honest errors on the xenon-alone values are ×4.6 larger (70 ± 19 … 105 ± 32) before adding isoflurane-ED50 error.
- Quantal ED50 simulation, n=20: SE(ED50) = 1.9 / 3.9 / 5.8 for threshold SD 5 / 10 / 15 with one dose per mouse; 1.1 / 2.5 / 3.8 with five doses per mouse. An SE of 4–7 would need a threshold SD of 10–18 points on a mean of 15–23, implausible; another reason ± is SD.

## Missed experiments

**1. Randomised-readout brain–computer interface (best).** Record a population in an awake, rewarded animal. After each trial's activity x is stored, a random-number generator draws the decoder w; reward if w·x > 0. By symmetry P = ½ exactly for any forward-causal brain, whatever its noise, chemistry or correlations. Arm 2: w drawn before the trial but hidden. Arm 3: w fixed and learnable (positive control; ordinary learning).

- In the repo's own network (N=200, sustained goal-only chooser, 5 steps): teleological chooser scores 0.795 / 0.577 / 0.528 at δ = 0.05 / 0.01 / 0.003; a chooser that does not know w scores 0.51 / 0.51 / 0.50 (SE 0.008, 0.008, 0.0025) [C].
- Sensitivity: 10⁵ trials (100 sessions) → trial-level shift 7.9×10⁻³ at 5σ. Mapped to per-event bias with Scenario 4's pooling: ε ≈ 7×10⁻⁵ (10⁴ events), 7×10⁻⁷ (10⁸), 2×10⁻¹⁰ (10¹⁵) [C]. That covers the window counting cannot reach, on biological events, with no baseline problem.
- Meaning: a null removes A and B. A positive cannot be mimicked by neuromodulation; confounds are leakage of w and optional stopping, both controllable. C survives by construction, which is the point of finding 13.
- Free first pass: any public dataset where reward was randomised after the choice.

**2. Branching ratio of a nuclear decay inside the awake brain.** A positron emitter with a prompt gamma line [R: e.g. ⁸⁹Zr or ²²Na] in a head-fixed animal: the ratio of 511 keV to gamma-line counts is fixed by nuclear physics and independent of tracer amount. Cycle awake ↔ anaesthetised. ~10⁴ counts/s per line → 10⁹ per day → ~3×10⁻⁴ differential in a day [C]. Tests only a blanket rule change in awake tissue, not an aimed chooser. Endogenous ⁴⁰K (~130 Bq in a human brain [R]) is too weak.
