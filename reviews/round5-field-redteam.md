**Bottom line.** The table supports a modest claim: four crude scalars do not single out brains. The three verdicts do not follow. "Eliminated" quantifies over an untested set; "not available without breaking locality" is wrong physics; "live, and testable" rests on a signature table that mixes two incompatible kinds of transition and is contradicted on one row by the literature.

## Item 6 — what the scenario may conclude

1. Mass, heat output, stored field energy and a loosely defined event rate do not distinguish a brain from a liver, a kettle or a chip. "Eliminated" must go.
2. Nothing about locality. Withdraw.
3. Anaesthetic transitions in animals behave like a bistable switch. That is a fact about arousal dynamics, shared with seizures, and no test of sentience as an order parameter.
4. "Sentience as an emergent field" is at present a metaphor that makes no prediction a rival would not.
