# Round 18 adjudication — Scenario 33 (Amendment governs)

Briefs 28 [awareness], 29 [feeling]; mostly abstract-only.

## 1. Abilities (W = without awareness; L = lost without it; CO = co-occurrence; F = function evidence)

- a trace learning: W no; L not established (delay conditioning tracks awareness too). CO.
- b holding: W yes, small (19 labs). F null.
- c chaining, d novel integration: W no (replications failed); L leans yes. CO.
- e control: W partial (slowing only). F null.
- f trade-offs: untested. g: not gathered.
- h priming: weaker than textbooks say.
- i metacognition: L in blindsight at matched performance (n=1); partly survives in normals. F.
- j initiating action: L leans yes (one study).
- k durable memory: W implicit only, one lab.
- l novelty: L leans yes. CO; one F case (exclusion).

## 2. Accounts

- **E**: unscoreable.

## 3. C [feeling]

(a) **C.1 has no direct test.** Adipsia and congenital pain insensitivity show that losing the need signal is dangerous and that knowledge compensates only partly. Signal and feeling vanish together, so neither case says which does the work.

(b) **C.2: undecided, lean contradicted.** A 302-neuron worm makes graded, hunger-dependent threat-versus-food trade-offs meeting the Amendment's definition of f (Ghosh 2016, full text, unreplicated); slime mould, bacteria and learning agents give partial analogues. C may not narrow "trade-off" afterwards nor credit the worm with feeling without an independent sign. Trade-offs alone therefore do not mark feeling, and a common currency can exist with no evidence of feeling. Mammals' implementation is not shown.

(c) Bee: disputed, unresolved. Hermit crab: fragile. Shore crab: one study. Octopus: independent signs, no trade-off test.

(d) Cabanac: lean at most; one lab, n=3–12, unreplicated; animal pleasure defined by choice.

(e) Where rating and choice diverge, the value signal follows choice while ratings update normally (Reber, Howard; Hare snippet-only). Lean toward readout; necessity untouched.

C.3, C-strong: untested.

## 4. Overall

No evidence shows a job needing feeling rather than an unfelt value signal; none shows feeling idle. For awareness, the tentative candidate is self-monitoring (H). The owner's question stays open: need signals plainly aid survival; whether their being felt adds anything is untested.

## 5. Missing studies

1. **Feeling blunted, signal intact.** Preregistered, n≥100: cold pain against food at graded hunger; hypnotic or opioid reduction of unpleasantness, intensity ratings and option-awareness verified. C: exchange rate follows unpleasantness. Readout: follows intensity and need. A: follows awareness of urges.
2. **Matched-signal awareness, multi-lab.** Equal discrimination, differing visibility; test chaining, integration, exclusion, metacognition. N: no gain. G: c, d gain. H: metacognition only.

## PERMITTED

1. "No study has yet tested trade-offs in people whose feeling is reduced while the body's signal is intact."
2. "People who cannot feel thirst or pain are in real danger, but they have lost the body's signal as well as the feeling."
3. "A worm with 302 nerve cells weighs food against danger and shifts the balance as it gets hungrier."
4. "So weighing unlike needs cannot, by itself, be taken as a sign of feeling."
5. "Several headline claims of sophisticated unconscious thinking have not held up when repeated."
6. "The best current candidate for what awareness adds is judging how reliable your own perception is; this rests on very few cases."
7. "The evidence that bees and hermit crabs make such trade-offs is disputed or fragile."
8. "We found no evidence of a job that requires feeling rather than an unfelt value signal, and none that feeling does nothing."
9. "Whether feeling helps survival beyond what an equally clever unfeeling system could manage remains unanswered."

## FORBIDDEN

- "Feeling exists to weigh competing needs." / "Feeling has no function."
- "Feeling is needed for a common currency."
- "The worm shows humans do not need feeling." / "Worms feel."
- "Trade-offs prove bees or crabs feel pain."
- "Without thirst people die, so feeling is essential."
- "Consciousness is for self-monitoring."
- "An automaton would survive just as well."
