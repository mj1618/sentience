# Round 5 — Independent adjudication of Scenario 11

Independent adjudicator, 2026-10-03.

**Bottom line.** The evidence barely moves credence. The rows that could have separated D from C (1, 3, 4) are empty at the decisive point. What did come back damages only a version of C that its proponents do not hold.

## 1. The predictions themselves

**D** is stated fairly; Panksepp, Solms and Merker would sign it. Two problems. The row 3 carve-out ("triggers such as the amygdala") is legitimate Panksepp, but it exempted in advance the only deep structure for which good data exist. And rows 1 and 5 pair "feeling present" with "concern/worry reduced", so any report of feeling counts as a pass.

**C is not stated fairly.** It is a composite that no named proponent holds.
- LeDoux & Brown and Lau locate the higher-order step mainly in lateral PFC, and Lau argues explicitly that small surviving PFC tissue suffices (Odegaard 2017). They would not predict abolition from partial, medial or orbital lesions.
- Dehaene's workspace is a distributed fronto-parietal-cingulate network, robust to focal loss, and is not a theory of valence.
- No C theorist predicts weak dream emotion. REM lateral PFC is hypoactive, not off, and medial PFC/ACC are active (Maquet 2005). The lucid-dream prediction is the authors' invention.
- Cingulotomy lesions the ACC, not the lateral network. Row 5's C cell fits an "ACC/insula makes it unpleasant" theory, not higher-order theory.

**R** is "present, possibly altered" in every row. It could fail only if one structure proved decisive, which no row could show.

**Discriminating rows:** 3 (PAG/hypothalamus only) and 4 genuinely; 1 only for near-total bilateral lateral PFC loss; 2 weakly. Row 5 separates D from a strawman C. Row 6 and extras 7 and 9 could not have failed for anyone.

No CONFIRMED and no CONTRADICTED anywhere.

**Row 1.** The specified situation never occurred with a feeling report. Boly and Odegaard [V-full] concern level of consciousness, and the classic cases spared substantial PFC. Brickner's emotion data are [R] and observer-only, so discounted to zero. What exists: Roger (n=1, [V-full], real-time ratings) and Patient B (n=1, mostly observational) feel after medial/orbital/insular/cingulate loss with lateral PFC spared. Gillihan [V-full] shows more self-rated sadness after unilateral lateral lesions (n=8). This strains medial or limbic-cortex versions of C and leaves lateral-PFC C untouched. D gets no more than CONSISTENT because its decisive condition was not met.

**Row 2.** Emotion is frequent in REM reports (Fosse [V-abs], 74% of 88; Sikka 2019 [V-full]) while lateral PFC is relatively hypoactive ([V-abs] PET, n=7–8). Sikka 2019 runs opposite to C-as-written (less right-frontal activity, more anger), but it is alpha asymmetry, trait-like, n=17 and one emotion. No study measured emotional intensity against frontal activity within subjects, and none measured emotion in lucid dreams, so D's "regardless" is untested.
Memory confound: forgetting would under-count emotion, so presence is robust. But every rating was made awake, with frontal cortex back online, so "felt during the dream" cannot be separated from "judged felt on waking". That is the loophole C needs.

**Row 3.** Inman 2020 [V-full, checked]: sham-controlled, blinded, probed after each stimulation; 7 of 9 report no emotion despite dose-dependent autonomic change. It is solid, with minimal memory exposure, but it is amygdala, which D pre-exempted and C predicted. Both pass and nothing discriminates. It mildly strains R's "rare and partial".
Neudorfer 2021 [V-full, checked] is not a clean case against D. It is a retrospective side-effect log; feeling was not queried; the patients have Alzheimer's (report and memory confound severe); the target was fornix; the denominator is ambiguous. And "warmth" (24.6%) is itself a reported feeling.
PAG: Green [V-abs] is silent on feeling; Nashold is [R]. The generator test did not happen.

**Row 4.** Only stimulus-locked latencies exist. Nothing relates feeling onset to either site.

**Row 5.** Two n=1 cingulotomy cases (Greenspan [V-full]; Davis [V-abs]) show ratings unchanged or increased, and Roger agrees. The lobotomy "hurts but doesn't bother me" record, the origin of D's prediction, is [R] and unverified, so it scores nothing. Berthier [V-abs] (asymbolia after insular lesions) points the other way, but it is observer-described, the patients' words are [R], and Roger (bilateral insula loss) contradicts it. C-as-written is strained; real C made no such prediction.

**Row 8.** Fear seizures involve orbitofrontal, lateral prefrontal and ACC (Biraben; Zhang 2026, both [V-abs]; Zhang not independently verified). Brainstem and hypothalamus are not sampled and there is no deep-only contrast, so this is a faint co-occurrence point for C and R.

**Row 10.** rTMS to dlPFC left pain threshold and tolerance unchanged but blocked placebo analgesia (Krummenacher [V-abs]). The perturbation is weak, focal and unilateral, and unpleasantness was not the measure. It fits "lateral PFC regulates" better than "lateral PFC constitutes", mildly.

**Rows 6, 7, 9.** Non-discriminating, or retrospective recall only.

## 3. Overall judgement

- **D vs C as written:** slight shift toward D, roughly 1.5:1 at most.
- **D vs C as its proponents state it:** none to negligible (about 1.1:1). Every strained cell is strained by the unfair wording.
- **D vs R:** none. R absorbed everything, which is a defect of R.

"C is in trouble" is not supported: in no case here was the lateral frontal network shown to be gone or off.

**Most informative finding:** Roger's cold-pressor ratings (Feinstein 2016). Checked against full text: 10/10 on three of four trials, 8.7 on the fourth; above the controls' 75th percentile; continuous real-time ratings plus concurrent vocalisation, face, withdrawal and autonomic measures. It is the one result where the memory confound does not apply. It is solid as an observation, but n=1 and one modality. It removes "insula/ACC/amygdala/medial PFC are necessary for felt unpleasantness" and says nothing about lateral PFC.

Second: Inman 2020. Equally solid, but uninformative between accounts because of D's carve-out.

## 4. Experiments feasible today

1. **Blinded PAG and hypothalamic stimulation with immediate feeling probes.** Use patients already implanted in PAG/PVG (chronic pain) or posterior hypothalamus (cluster headache). Apply Inman's protocol: sham control, stepwise amplitude, autonomic recording, a structured valence and intensity probe after every trial, and a continuous rating dial. Add scalp EEG or externalised-lead LFP to obtain feeling-onset timing. D predicts dose-dependent feeling whenever autonomic output is driven, locked to deep activity. C predicts autonomic-without-feeling trials, with feeling tracking fronto-parietal recruitment. This fills rows 3 and 4 together.
2. **Within-subject felt versus not-felt contrast in SEEG patients.** Repeat amygdala (or insula/ACC) stimulation at fixed amplitude in patients who also have prefrontal contacts. Test whether prefrontal propagation (evoked potentials, high-gamma) separates trials with a reported feeling from trials without. Inman's two responders had electrode sites resembling the non-responders', so the difference lies downstream. C predicts it is prefrontal; D predicts spread to hypothalamus/PAG; R predicts neither alone.

A cheaper third: Siclari-style serial awakenings with an immediate emotion-intensity rating. It fills row 2 but keeps the waking-rating confound.

## 5. Where the result is uninterpretable

- **R, every row.** No outcome could have counted against it.
- **Row 1.** "Largely lost" set no threshold and did not separate lateral from medial. Each side can claim the same cases.
- **Row 2.** "Weak" and "intense" had no scale and no waking baseline; "largely switched off" overstated the physiology.
- **Row 3.** The generator/trigger split lets D reassign any null to the wrong site, wrong parameters or "merely a trigger". Neudorfer is exactly that case.
- **Row 5.** Lobotomy, leucotomy and cingulotomy were lumped although they cut different things; "altered, not abolished" fits any change in either direction.
- **"Full-strength feeling".** No measure of strength was specified.
