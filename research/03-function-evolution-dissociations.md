# 03 — Function, evolution and dissociations of sentience

Compiled 2026-10-03. Working hypothesis under test: **consciousness = sentience (felt valence, the motivator) + thought (works out how to satisfy it)**.

## A. Does sentience do anything?

**Evolutionary argument against epiphenomenalism.** James (1879, 1890): if feelings were causally idle, selection could not explain why pleasure tracks benefit and pain tracks harm; the alignment implies feelings have effects. [S] https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/philosophy-of-science/article/william-jamess-objection-to-epiphenomenalism/12F99541148B2C6CCACAB84D0B0B5EAF
- Robinson's reply ("James's Evolutionary Argument", Disputatio 2014): calls the argument formidable but not forcing; selection acts on the *neural causes* of pleasure/pain, and the feeling rides along by psychophysical law. [S] https://reference-global.com/download/article/10.2478/disp-2014-0014.pdf (repository copy returned 403)
- Corabi critiques James's version and offers a repaired one; Mørch recasts it as an argument for "phenomenal powers" (pain's feel *makes* avoidance intelligible); Wright offers a rival reading. [S] https://www.philarchive.org/rec/MRCTEA , https://dc2.philarchive.org/rec/WRIPPO
- Status: the argument defeats only views on which the feeling–function pairing is arbitrary. It does not separate interactionism from identity theory/functionalism. Search summary: "widely criticized and few have deemed it cogent" [S].

**Paradox of phenomenal judgment / meta-problem.** Chalmers (1996, ch. 5): our *reports* about consciousness are physical events, so they must have a physical explanation that never mentions consciousness — awkward for any non-reductive view. [R] Chalmers (2018, JCS) generalises this as the meta-problem: explain "problem intuitions". [S] https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/publications/the-meta-problem-of-consciousness/
- Bearing: if sentience is a causal motivator it must sit inside the causal chain that produces reports about it. The hypothesis is committed to non-epiphenomenalism and owes an account of how.

**Functional proposals**
- *Common currency* — Cabanac 1992 (J Theor Biol 155:173–200): pleasure/displeasure is the single scale on which incommensurable motivations (hunger, fatigue, cold, pain, money) are traded off. [S] https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1186/1471-244X-2-7 (Cabanac 2002 restatement)
- *Value makes experience functional* — Cleeremans & Tallon-Baudry 2022: experience has intrinsic value, acting as a "mental currency" that lets agents compare disparate experiences in one subject-centred space. [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9036654
- *Unlimited Associative Learning* — Ginsburg & Jablonka: open-ended learning that assigns motivational value to novel compound stimuli/actions is the evolutionary transition marker of minimal consciousness (a marker, not a mechanism). [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7116763
- *Homeostatic feelings* — Damasio & Carvalho 2013: feelings are experienced read-outs of body state that make homeostatic need matter to the organism. [R] https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn3403
- *Felt uncertainty* — Solms 2019: "consciousness is felt uncertainty"; unpleasure = rising uncertainty about a homeostatic need, pleasure = things going as expected; its job is "life-preserving intentional behavior in unpredicted situations"; generated in the upper brainstem; "experience itself is not a cortical function". [V] https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02714/full
- *Supramodular Interaction Theory* — Morsella 2005: conscious states are needed only to integrate conflicting action tendencies competing for skeletal muscle (e.g. holding breath, enduring pain). [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC549495 (URL from search; not confirmed to be this paper)
- *Credit assignment*: no single canonical source found arguing felt valence is required for credit assignment; the idea is implicit in UAL and in Section D. Not verified.

Convergence: five independent proposals put the function at *prioritising among incommensurable needs in novel situations*. That is the "motivator" half of the hypothesis.

## B. Dissociation evidence

### B1. Sentience without cortex/thought
- Merker 2007 (BBS 30:63–81): upper brainstem integrates target selection, action selection and motivation; children with hydranencephaly show responsiveness and emotion. [S] https://mbscience.org/?p=819
- Aleman & Merker 2014 (Acta Paediatrica 103:1057–65): survey of 108 primary caregivers, 106 questions; composite behaviour "incompatible on multiple counts with the unconsciousness characteristic of the vegetative state"; survival to decades (oldest 35 in a 357-person sample). [V] https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/neuroscience/anencephaly/2014-aleman.pdf
- Berridge & Kringelbach 2015 also cite hydranencephalic children's emotional responses as evidence subcortical structures suffice for basic pleasure. [V] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4425246/
- Panksepp: neonatally decorticated rats play indistinguishably from controls; subcortical stimulation (PAG, hypothalamus) is rewarding/punishing and evokes emotional action patterns across mammals. [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7219686 Human PAG-stimulation reports of dread/pain: [R], no source fetched.
- Anencephalic infants: no source found this session. [R]
- **Critiques** (BBS commentaries): remnant cortex in hydranencephaly; Coenen — brainstem gives wakefulness, phenomenal content needs thalamocortex; Collerton & Perry — definition so broad nothing with a brain is excluded; Morin — excludes reflective thought so not surprising. [S] https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/consciousness-is-more-than-wakefulness/073967D01D85F68B2C83451B99FC6E04 , https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/abs/grounding-consciousness-the-mesodiencephalon-as-thalamocortical-base/DCA9B487F7EC7DA844F9E869D3138CE7
- **Solidity: moderate.** Behaviour is robust; inference to *felt* affect rests on caregiver report and behavioural analogy, with no self-report. Stimulation evidence shows subcortical *sufficiency to trigger*, not that cortex contributes nothing to the feeling.

### B2. Cognition without sentience
- Mudrik & Deouell 2022 (Annu Rev Neurosci 45:403–23), best current neural review: "initial support for processing object categories and words, possibly to the semantic level, as well as emotional expressions. Robust neural evidence for face individuation and integration of sentences or scenes is lacking." Unseen stimuli can capture attention exogenously "but not endogenously". Inconsistency traced to weak awareness controls. [V] https://people.socsci.tau.ac.il/mu/mudriklab/files/2023/08/ANR_2023.pdf
  - Reading: unconscious processing is real but shallow, local and non-integrative. Novel combination, multi-step integration and endogenous control are not demonstrated without awareness.
- Blindsight: classic performance-without-awareness (Weiskrantz) is contested. Phillips 2021 (Psych Review) argues it is "qualitatively degraded conscious vision" masked by conservative report criteria; replies defend a *relative* dissociation. [S] https://perception.jhu.edu/files/PDFs/Misc/Phillips_Blindsight_PsychRev.pdf
- Sleepwalking: complex behaviour (cooking, dressing, playing an instrument) from slow-wave sleep (Zadra et al. 2013, Lancet Neurol 12:285–94); one SPECT case showed frontoparietal deactivation with cingulate/cerebellar activation (Bassetti 2000). [S] https://www.em-consulte.com/article/788300/somnambulism-clinical-aspects-and-pathophysiologic
- Absence seizures: selective loss of consciousness with simple behaviours and orienting sometimes preserved; impairment graded. Blumenfeld 2012. [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3432285
- Caveat for the hypothesis: these show loss of *access/report*, not specifically loss of *valence*. None is a clean "thought, zero affect" case.

### B3. Affect dissociated from sensation
- **Pain asymbolia.** Berthier, Starkstein & Leiguarda 1988: 6 patients, insula damaged in all; intact pain recognition but "lack of withdrawal and absent or inadequate emotional responses" to pain and to threat. [S] https://stafforini.com/works/berthier-1988-asymbolia-pain-sensorylimbic/ Klein (Mind 2015): they lost a general capacity to *care about bodily integrity*, not a pain component. [S] https://researchportalplus.anu.edu.au/en/publications/what-pain-asymbolia-really-shows/ Either way: remove the affect and protective behaviour goes, with sensation and intellect intact.
- **Morphine.** Price et al. 1985: low doses (0.04–0.06 mg/kg) reduced unpleasantness but not sensory intensity. [S] https://stafforini.com/works/price-1985-psychophysical-analysis-morphine/
- **Cingulotomy.** Foltz & White 1962: pain still present but "less bothersome". [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11522883 Lobotomy pain reports: [R], not sourced.
- **Congenital insensitivity to pain (CIP) — survival data.**
  - CIPA (NTRK1/HSAN-IV): "Death from hyperpyrexia occurs within the first 3 years of life in almost 20% of the patients" (Rosemberg et al. 1994, Pediatr Neurol; repeated in Schulman et al. 2001). [V, abstracts via Europe PMC] https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=%22insensitivity%20to%20pain%22%20AND%20anhidrosis%20AND%20%22first%203%20years%22&resultType=core&format=json
  - **Confound:** those deaths are from anhidrosis (cannot sweat), not painlessness. The 20% figure must not be cited as the cost of lacking pain.
  - Systematic review of 29 CIPA case reports (2014–2024), mean age 10.9: fever 87%, self-mutilation 73%, recurrent fractures 68%, developmental delay 63%; follow-up too poor for long-term outcomes. [V, abstract] https://apicareonline.com/index.php/APIC/article/view/3067
  - SCN9A/Nav1.7 CIP (pain loss without anhidrosis, normal intellect): "experience more injuries and may have shorter life expectancies"; unnoticed fractures, joint deformity. [V] https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/congenital-insensitivity-to-pain/ Index case (Cox et al. 2006, Nature): boy who walked on coals died at 14 jumping off a roof. [S] https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17167479/
  - **No actuarial life-expectancy figure for pure-painlessness CIP was found.** A "43% lifetime mortality with sepsis" figure appeared in a search summary with no traceable source — do not use.
  - Interpretation: CIP removes the nociceptive *input*, so it shows the pain system is fitness-critical and that intact intelligence cannot substitute for it. It does not isolate the *feeling*; asymbolia is the cleaner test.
- **Wanting vs liking.** Berridge & Kringelbach 2015 (Neuron): dopamine drives incentive salience, not pleasure — "dopamine is not actually needed for the hedonic impact of food pleasure, but rather only for their incentive motivation value". Liking depends on small opioid/endocannabinoid hotspots (~1 mm³ in rat) in accumbens, ventral pallidum, brainstem. [V] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4425246/
  - **Direct problem for "sentience = the motivator":** motivation can be raised without raising felt pleasure, and in addiction wanting grows as liking falls.
- **Anhedonia.** Treadway & Zald 2011: clinical anhedonia conflates consummatory (liking) and motivational (wanting) deficits; in depression the deficit is often motivational with liking spared. [S] https://jimhopper.com/pdf/treadway_zald_2011.pdf

### B4. Motivation loss with intact cognition
- **Auto-activation deficit / athymhormia.** Laplane & Dubois 2001 (Mov Disord 16:810–14), read in full. [V] https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/2001-laplane.pdf
  - Bilateral basal ganglia (pallidal/caudate) or striato-thalamo-prefrontal lesions. Patients sit all day, take "no initiatives", yet "answer questions appropriately"; WAIS and Raven's "generally normal"; "mental potential … virtually unchanged but is not spontaneously mobilized".
  - Mind is "empty, a total blank": "no thoughts and no projections in the future", and they are not bored.
  - Affect flattened: "express neither fear nor desire"; events "lack 'resonance'".
  - Fully reversed by external prompting; one patient played high-level bridge when stimulated. A man stood 45 minutes with hands on a lawnmower until his son told him to move.
  - One catatonia-like patient was unresponsive to verbal commands but responded to *emotional* stimuli (mother's voice, her dog, her horse): "only emotion could produce a response".
  - Authors: "conscious awareness without any content, i.e., consciousness per se."
- **Akinetic mutism.** Damasio & Van Hoesen's bicingulate/SMA patient, after recovery, said she "had nothing to say" and an "empty mind" — as reported in Laplane & Dubois. [V, secondary] External prompting was largely ineffective there. The popular "nothing mattered" quotation could not be traced.
- Abulia is the milder end of the same spectrum. [R]
- **Strongest support for sentience-as-motivator**: remove drive/affect and spontaneous thought stops although the machinery works when driven from outside. Two complications: (a) affect and drive vanish *together*, so affect may be co-victim rather than cause; (b) waking awareness persists with neither affect nor thought.

## C. Phylogenetic distribution
- **NY Declaration (19 Apr 2024):** "strong scientific support" for mammals and birds; "at least a realistic possibility of conscious experience in all vertebrates … and many invertebrates (including, at minimum, cephalopod mollusks, decapod crustaceans, and insects)". 611 signatories when fetched. [V] https://sites.google.com/nyu.edu/nydeclaration/declaration
- **Birch et al. 2021 (LSE report):** eight criteria, >300 papers; "very strong" evidence in octopods, "strong" in true crabs; basis for UK law. [S] https://www.lse.ac.uk/business/consulting/reports/review-of-the-evidence-of-sentiences-in-cephalopod-molluscs-and-decapod-crustaceans
- **Decapod trade-offs:** hermit crabs in preferred shells "evacuated at a higher shock level than those in poor quality shells" (Appel & Elwood 2009, Appl Anim Behav Sci). [V, abstract] https://pure.qub.ac.uk/en/publications/motivational-trade-offs-and-potential-pain-experience-in-hermit-c/ Shocked crabs later moved faster to a new shell (Elwood & Appel 2009, Anim Behav 77:1243). [S] https://marineinfo.org/id/publication/212632
- **Fish:** trout have nociceptors; acid-induced behaviour change is reversed by morphine (Sneddon 2003). [S] https://fishcount.org.uk/fish-welfare-in-commercial-fishing/fish-sentience/fish-feel-pain Counter: Key 2016, "Why fish do not feel pain" (no cortex). [R]
- **Insects:** bumblebees roll balls with no reward, meeting play criteria (Galpayage Dona et al. 2022). [S] https://oulurepo.oulu.fi/handle/10024/43665 Shaken honeybees classify ambiguous odours pessimistically (Bateson et al. 2011). [S] https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21636277/ Gibbons et al. 2022: strong evidence for pain in adult Diptera and Blattodea on the Birch criteria. [S] https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/117533
- **Anaesthesia:** anaesthetics act "from single-celled paramecia to primates", via cytoskeleton, ion channels and mitochondria; proposed general definition is "isolation of an organism from its environment" (Kelz & Mashour 2019, Curr Biol). [V, abstract] https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/search?query=ext_id:31743680&resultType=core&format=json Draguhn, Mallatt & Robinson (Protoplasma): because plants and bacteria respond, sensitivity "does not indicate consciousness". [V] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7907021/
- Pattern: trade-off behaviour and affect-like biases appear wherever there is centralised integration plus flexible learning, across at least three independently evolved complex brains (vertebrate, arthropod, cephalopod). Anaesthetic sensitivity is not diagnostic.

## D. Reinforcement learning and "reward ≠ felt valence"
- Singh, Lewis & Barto 2009 (CogSci, pp. 2601–06): searching over reward functions by fitness shows the optimal reward function "need not bear a transparent relationship to an agent's ultimate goal"; many outperform the task-specific reward. Primary rewards are evolved proxies. [S] https://public.websites.umich.edu/~rickl/ai-rl.html
  - Bearing: reproduces James's alignment (reward tracks fitness) with no feeling in the model. So the alignment alone does not show feeling is causal.
- Tomasik 2014: present RL agents have "a very small but nonzero degree of ethical importance". [V, abstract] https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.8233v1
- A scalar reward is not obviously valence: its sign and zero point are arbitrary under transformations that leave behaviour unchanged. [R — standard argument; no primary source fetched]
- Long, Sebo et al. 2024, "Taking AI Welfare Seriously": separates consciousness from "robust agency" as routes to moral status. [S] https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00986v1
- Biology already splits the two: dopamine prediction-error ("wanting") is the RL-like signal; "liking" is a separate opioid system (B3).

## Constraints this yields

1. **[strong]** Sensory discrimination and affective unpleasantness are separable (asymbolia, low-dose morphine, cingulotomy). Valence is added to sensation, not intrinsic to it.
2. **[strong]** Motivation ("wanting") and hedonic feeling ("liking") are separable in mechanism and can move in opposite directions. Any theory must say which one "sentience" names.
3. **[strong]** Intact intelligence does not replace the pain system: CIP patients accumulate injuries, fractures and self-mutilation. (Mortality figures are confounded by anhidrosis; pure-CIP life expectancy is unquantified.)
4. **[strong]** Losing endogenous drive abolishes spontaneous thought and action while leaving cognitive capacity intact under external prompting (auto-activation deficit).
5. **[moderate]** Unconscious processing reaches single-item semantics and emotion but not integration across items, novel combination or endogenous control.
6. **[moderate]** Affect-like behaviour survives near-total cortical loss; the upper brainstem/diencephalon suffices for emotional behaviour. Whether it suffices for *feeling* is contested.
7. **[moderate]** Motivational trade-offs and affect-like biases occur in decapods, cephalopods, insects and fish; a theory tied to mammalian cortex must explain them away.
8. **[moderate]** A fitness-aligned reward signal can be built with no feeling (evolved RL rewards), so the pleasure–benefit alignment does not by itself prove feeling is causal.
9. **[moderate]** The theory must place sentience inside the causal chain that produces reports about it (paradox of phenomenal judgment).
10. **[weak]** Wakeful awareness may persist with neither affect nor thought content (AAD "empty mind"), resting on retrospective self-report.
11. **[weak]** Anaesthetic sensitivity is universal to cells and cannot mark sentience.

## Bearing on consciousness = sentience + thought

**For**
- AAD/akinetic mutism: when drive and affect go, thought stops, yet the thinking machinery is intact — what the "motivator + problem-solver" split predicts. Emotional stimuli reactivated a patient whom commands could not.
- Asymbolia: sensation and intellect intact, affect gone, protective behaviour gone.
- CIP: cognition alone cannot do the job of the pain system.
- Decorticate/hydranencephalic evidence: affect-like states appear without the cortical substrate of thought, as a two-component view allows.
- Cabanac, Solms, Damasio, Cleeremans and Morsella converge on affect as the prioritiser.
- Trade-off behaviour across phyla tracks flexible learning, consistent with valence being what flexible cognition serves.

**Against / complications**
- Wanting ≠ liking: the motivator is dissociable from the feeling. "Sentience is the motivator" must either identify sentience with wanting (not obviously felt) or concede that liking is not the proximal driver.
- AAD patients report awareness with *neither* affect nor thought — a residual third ingredient (arousal/wakefulness) the equation omits.
- In AAD, affect and drive are lost together from basal ganglia damage; affect may be co-victim, not cause.
- Unconscious-processing evidence concerns perceptual *access*, an axis orthogonal to valence. Blindsight suggests neutral perceptual experience is a component that is neither affect nor thought.
- Evolved-reward RL shows a functional motivator with no evident feeling; the hypothesis needs to say what feeling adds beyond a reward scalar.
- Subcortical sentience evidence is behavioural; critics hold it shows wakefulness plus emotional *behaviour* only.
- The evolutionary argument does not discriminate the hypothesis from identity theory or functionalism.

**Sharpest open question:** is the motivator the *felt* state (liking/unpleasantness) or an unfelt incentive-salience signal that feeling normally accompanies? Asymbolia favours the first; Berridge's dissociation favours the second.
