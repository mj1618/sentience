# 12 — Four conjectures: rate, ownership, slow medium, no-policy

Literature brief, compiled 2026-10-03.

**Tags.** [V-full] = full text read this session. [V-abs] = abstract read (Europe PMC API). [R] = recalled, not checked.

**Limits.** No web search; Europe PMC + PMC only. PMC full text was blocked (reCAPTCHA) for Rutledge 2014 and Banzett 2021, so both are abstract-level. No abstract exists in Europe PMC for Bejjani 1999, Kamin/Brimer/Black 1963, Moseley 2008 or Cabanac 1971 beyond a summary; Hsee & Abelson 1991 and Lawrence/Carver/Scheier 2002 were not found. Nothing was found on felt pain in somatoparaphrenia or xenomelia, on multi-day hunger ratings, on ketamine, or on expert-vs-novice affect. Sub-second latency of *felt* valence was not found measured anywhere.

## 1 — Rate, not level

*Felt good/bad tracks the rate of change of a regulated need, not the size of the deficit.*

### For

- **Offset analgesia.** A 1–3 °C fall from 47–50 °C changed pain ratings by up to 271% more than an equal rise, and was indistinguishable from a 15 °C fall. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/11929939
  - Yelle 2008 (n = 13 and 10): after 50→49 °C, minimum pain was 72% lower than at constant 49 °C, for about 15 s; ratings fell faster than the stimulus. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2213795/
- **Momentary happiness.** Happiness in a gambling task was "explained not by current task earnings" but by recent expectations and prediction errors; replicated in 18,420 app users. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/25092308
  - Mood proposed as "momentum of recent outcomes" — a theory paper, not a test. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/26545853
- **Drinking.** Oral fluid "rapidly abolished thirst independent of pOsm". [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5763530/
  - Fits a predicted-change signal, not the measured derivative: osmolality had not yet moved.
- **Thermal pleasure** is reported during temperature step-changes and is predictable from sensation change. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/33736141
- **Alliesthesia.** Same stimulus pleasant or unpleasant by internal state. [V-abs, summary only] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/5098954
  - This is a state-dependence (level) result as much as a rate result: pleasure depends on *being* in deficit.
- **Relief** pleasantness scales with violated negative expectation (n = 18). [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/21490964
- Lottery winners no happier than controls (n = 22 each). [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/690806
- Hsee & Abelson 1991; Lawrence, Carver & Scheier 2002: satisfaction follows velocity of progress. [R] — not retrieved.

### Against

- **Thirst follows level.** Systematic review, 12 trials, 167 people: threshold 285.2 mOsm/kg, slope 0.54 cm VAS per mOsm/kg, mean within-subject r = 0.91, "the increase in thirst with pOsm is linear". [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5763530/
  - My inference, not the review's: most included trials infuse hypertonic saline at a fixed rate, so the derivative is roughly constant while thirst climbs. A rate-only signal would plateau.
  - Free-living water restriction is much noisier: r² = 0.09 with osmolality, 0.24 with body-mass loss (n = 18). [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7551505/
- **Air hunger follows level.** Under sustained hypoxia it "tracked ventilatory drive with a delay of approximately 30 s" (n = 10). [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/15286044
  - Banzett's review describes a consistent stimulus–rating relation. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/33577128
- **Constant noxious heat does not fade to zero.** 46.5 °C for 2 min, n = 154: 86.4% showed peak, dip, then "a progressive increase". [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/26213476
  - Constant 49 °C for 30 s does adapt partly, but stays painful. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2213795/
- **Disability does not adapt.** Two panels (679 and 272 onset cases, followed 5–7 years): drops of 0.40–1.27 SD "followed by little adaptation". [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17469954
- Isolated decreases in noxious heat showed "no temporal contrast enhancement"; offset analgesia needs a preceding rise. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/30840535

### Verdict — **refuted as an exclusive claim; survives as one term**

Level is felt: thirst, air hunger and tonic heat all hold or rise at a held deficit. Rate (or prediction error) adds a large, well-replicated second term, strongest for relief and for reward mood. The Rutledge result concerns mood about money, not a regulated bodily need.

### Deciding experiment

End-tidal CO₂ clamp with fixed ventilation. Hold three PCO₂ levels flat for 10 min each, and ramp through the same levels at two rates, continuous unpleasantness rating. Regress rating on level and derivative. Rate-only predicts ratings decay to zero on every plateau.

## 2 — Ownership gate

*A signal is felt as good/bad only if bound to a model of one's own body/self.*

### For

- **Owned rubber hand under threat.** Insula/ACC activity as for the real hand, scaling with ownership strength. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17517605
  - "Injury" to an owned table or rubber hand gave strong SCR; weaker when asynchronous. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/12965016
- **Somatoparaphrenia.** Anticipatory SCR to noxious stimuli aimed at the disowned hand "significantly reduced" versus the other hand. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/24531623
  - Anticipatory SCR only. No rating of felt pain.
- **Hypnotic depersonalisation** (n = 7): lower pain intensity ratings; reduced somatosensory, parietal and amygdala response. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17230052
- **Depersonalisation disorder** (n = 15): reduced, slower SCR to unpleasant pictures, with faster SCR to a clap. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/12215083
- **Dissociation in BPD** (n = 12): pain threshold correlates with dissociation. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17126914
- **Full-body illusion** raised pressure-pain thresholds, correlated with self-identification. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/21570328 Avatar identification cut SCR to pain (n = 30). [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/24412686
- **Open-monitoring meditation.** Long-term meditators (n = 9, ≥10,000 h) rated unpleasantness, not intensity, lower than novices: F(1,17) = 7.71, p = .013. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2859822/
  - Four days' training: unpleasantness −57%, intensity −40%. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/21471390

### Against

- **No analgesia from the rubber hand illusion.** Two experiments (n = 16, 20), ownership confirmed in 15 of 16; no effect on pain ratings (p = .32), heat threshold (p = .17) or cold threshold (p = .65). [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3527497/
- **Every positive effect is graded.** Meditators' unpleasantness stayed above zero [V-full, Perlman above]; depersonalisation lowers ratings, it does not abolish them. A gate predicts all-or-none.
- **Ego dissolution carries strong affect.** Ego-dissolution scores correlate strongly with unitive experience (691 respondents, 1,828 drug experiences). [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/27378878
  - Challenging experience rises with psilocybin dose alongside mystical experience (n = 89). [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/37818448
  - "Dread of ego dissolution" is a standard scale in this literature. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/36373934
- **Direction problem.** In Hänsel and Romano, *gaining* identification with another body reduces pain. That is displacement or distraction as readily as disownership.
- Neonates and animals without mirror self-recognition show distress. [R]

### Verdict — **weakened; the gate form is unsupported, and partly unfalsifiable**

Ownership modulates threat and unpleasantness by modest, graded amounts, with one clean null. No retrieved study shows valence abolished by disownership. Ego dissolution with bliss or dread counts against it unless "self-model" is thinned to something that survives ego dissolution and exists in neonates — at which point nothing could refute it. The clinical cases that matter (felt pain in a disowned limb) were not found.

### Deciding experiment

Somatoparaphrenia patients with intact sensation in the affected limb. Matched noxious stimuli to both hands, intensity and unpleasantness rated separately, before and during caloric vestibular stimulation (reported to restore ownership transiently [R]). A gate predicts unpleasantness near zero when disowned and returning with ownership, intensity unchanged.

## 3 — Slow chemical medium

*Felt valence is the state of slow, diffuse neuromodulator/peptide signalling, not fast synaptic traffic.*

### For

- **Opioid blockade reduces pleasure.** Naloxone (0.15 mg/kg bolus + 0.2 mg/kg/h, n = 19) cut pleasure ratings for erotic pictures, T(18) = 3.90, at delivery and not anticipation. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6239433/
  - Naltrexone 50 mg (n = 15): music pleasure 6.33 → 5.78 on 0–10, d = 0.50; negative emotion blunted too. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5296903/
- **Time scale claimed for volume transmission:** "seconds-minutes", distances over 1 mm, versus milliseconds for wiring. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/23674109
- Air hunger lags drive by ~30 s [V-abs, above]; offset analgesia lasts ~15 s [V-full, above]. Both sit inside that window.

### Against

- **Blockade effects are small.** Half a scale point on music; pleasure reduced, never abolished, in either study. A medium that *is* valence should lose more when its main hedonic receptor is blocked.
- **Meditation analgesia is opioid-independent.** Naloxone "failed to reverse" it. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/26985045
- **Valence changes within seconds.** Pain ratings fall faster than the stimulus at offset. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2213795/
  - Amygdala stimulation induces reported fear, sadness or pleasant emotion. [V-abs; latency not given] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/16880223
  - STN stimulation produced hilarity in two patients. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/11746616
  - Bejjani 1999: sadness within seconds of switching current on, gone within about 90 s of switching off. [R] — record has no abstract. https://europepmc.org/article/MED/10320386
- Seconds-scale change is at the fast edge of the claimed volume-transmission range, so it strains the conjecture without breaking it.

### Verdict — **weakened on the opioid evidence; otherwise untested**

Opioids contribute a modest share of felt pleasure. Nothing retrieved tests "broad peptide blockade", and nothing measures whether felt valence can change in under a second. Affective priming and startle are fast but not shown to be felt. As stated ("slow" without a number) the conjecture is close to unfalsifiable; it needs a latency floor.

### Deciding experiment

Epilepsy patients with depth electrodes at a site that reliably evokes affect. Switch current on and off at random times; patient reports the moment feeling changes against a concurrent tone (temporal-order judgement). A reliable onset or offset latency under ~300 ms refutes a diffusion-limited medium; a floor of seconds supports it.

## 4 — No-policy signal

*Feeling marks situations with no automatised response and fades as a policy is learned.*

### For

- **Avoidance learning lowers arousal to the cue.** n = 23; SCR higher before than after the avoidance response was learned, t(22) = 4.73. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2762377/
  - After learning the shock was no longer delivered, so this is fear of the cue, not unpleasantness at matched input. No subjective ratings.
- Kamin, Brimer & Black 1963: fear of the CS falls with well-trained avoidance. [R] — no abstract. https://europepmc.org/article/MED/13962088
- **Self-controlled pain hurts less.** Identical stimuli, less pain and anxiety when self-controlled. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17079679
  - Perceived control attenuated ACC, insula and S2 response at constant stimulus. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/15306654
- **Repeated pain habituates.** Eight daily sessions, n = 20: "substantially decreased pain ratings to identical painful stimuli". [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17258858
  - Passive stimulation; no response was learned. Habituation without a policy cuts against the conjecture's specificity.

### Against

- **Control can increase pain.** n = 59, matched stimuli: controllable low-intensity heat rated lower (9.4 vs 18.5 unpredictable), but controllable high-intensity rated *higher* (68.7 vs 65.6). Control sharpened expectations; it did not reduce pain. [V-full] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12627469/
- **Extreme practice leaves the feeling intact.** Three of four elite breath-hold divers (6–9 min holds) had air hunger "within normal range". [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/17702673
- **Athletes.** 15 studies, 899 subjects: tolerance higher (g = 0.87), threshold difference not robust after excluding biased studies. [V-abs] https://europepmc.org/article/MED/22607985
  - They endure more of it; they do not feel less at onset.
- Eating and sex are overlearned and stay intensely pleasant; disability shows "little adaptation" despite years to build routines [V-abs, Lucas above]. First clause [R].

### Verdict — **weakened; holds for anticipatory fear, fails for consummatory feeling**

Fear of a warning cue falls as avoidance becomes reliable. No retrieved study shows unpleasantness of the stimulus itself falling with practice at matched input, and the divers and the Habermann data point the other way. Expert-versus-novice affect was not found.

### Deciding experiment

Train an escape response to noxious heat to automaticity over two weeks; yoked controls get identical heat with no contingency. On probe trials stimulus duration is fixed regardless of response. Compare unpleasantness ratings at matched input. The conjecture predicts a training-specific drop beyond the yoked group's habituation.
