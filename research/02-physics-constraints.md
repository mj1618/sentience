## 1. Carroll, "Consciousness and the Laws of Physics" (2021)

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07884 (ar5iv full text fetched) [V]

Argument (six steps): (1) low-energy physics is an effective QFT; (2) the Core Theory (SM + weak-field GR) fits all data in the "everyday-life regime", E ≤ 1e9 eV, and is tested to a cutoff ~1e11 eV; (3) any modification is either new dynamics of known fields or new fields; (4) the former violates EFT expectations and is experimentally constrained; (5) the latter must be too heavy, too weakly coupled, or too short-ranged to matter; (6) so the laws underlying everyday life are known. Crossing symmetry is the hinge of (5): if a new particle X couples to e/p/n strongly enough to push them around in a brain, the same amplitude rotated gives e⁺e⁻ → X X̄ (or X-mediated scattering), which colliders would have seen.

Loopholes Carroll concedes [V]: (a) non-local / configuration-dependent dynamics (particles in a brain obey different rules than the same particles elsewhere) — outside EFT; (b) modified quantum collapse dynamics; (c) non-physicalism. Standard criticisms [R]: the argument constrains *dynamics*, not intrinsic nature, so Russellian monism/panpsychism is untouched; it assumes locality and EFT decoupling, which strong-emergence and objective-collapse views deny by construction; "too weak to matter" is asserted against single-particle energetics, not against a coherent effect over N ~ 1e10–1e14 particles (which is exactly what must be computed against Section 2).

## 7. Consciousness-collapse (Chalmers & McQueen) and Kleiner–Hoel

Chalmers & McQueen, https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02314 [V]: consciousness (operationalised as IIT's Q-shape/Φ) is an approximately "super-resistant" observable. Absolute super-resistance fails by quantum Zeno (a system could never transition into consciousness), so they use CSL-type stochastic dynamics with Q-shape collapse operators, rate λ (free parameter, no value given), and correlation function G(x,y) = 1/EMD(x,y) between Q-shapes — larger Q-shape difference → faster collapse. Proposed tests: prepare small quantum-computer systems in superpositions of distinct Q-shape ("Schrödinger's dyad"; quantum Fredkin gate; ion-trap coherent feedback) and look for loss of interference beyond standard decoherence. No numerical prediction is given; the benchmark is ~2000-atom molecule interference. Related: Kremnizer–Ranchin Φ-CSL; McQueen & Okon on measures, https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13224 [Vs].

Kleiner & Hoel, "Falsification and consciousness", https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.03541 [V]: if a theory's prediction (from internal physical structure) and the experimenter's inference (from report/behaviour) are independent, one can substitute a system with identical input–output but different internals, falsifying any minimally informative theory (hits IIT hardest); if strictly dependent, the theory is unfalsifiable. Relevance here: any field/substrate theory whose "consciousness variable" can vary while behaviour is held fixed is exposed to the substitution argument; one that makes a *physical* difference (collapse rate, force, energy) escapes it — which is why Sections 2–4 matter.

## Constraints this yields

1. **Strong.** A new long-range (λ ≥ 40 µm) force coupled to ordinary matter must be weaker than gravity (α < 1 at 38.6 µm; ≲ 1e-3 for 1 mm–1 m). Gravity between neurons is ~1e-30 of the relevant electrical forces [G], so no such field can steer neural dynamics.
2. **Strong.** Any light (m < keV) scalar has g_e ≲ 7e-16, g_N ≲ 1e-12; pseudoscalar g_ae ≲ 1.3e-13, g_aN ≲ 3e-10. Exchange forces scale as g² — ≥ 22 orders below EM (α_EM = 7.3e-3).
3. **Strong.** Parameter-free Diósi–Penrose collapse is excluded (R₀ > 0.54 Å vs predicted 0.05 Å). Orch-OR must adopt a free-parameter or non-radiating collapse model, for which no quantitative prediction currently exists.
4. **Moderate.** In the 1 nm–1 µm window laboratory bounds are loose in gravity units (α ~ 1e21 → 1e7), but stellar cooling caps light mediators there; the remaining gap is mediators with m ≳ 10 keV (λ ≲ 0.01 nm, sub-atomic) or screened fields.
5. **Moderate.** Thermal decoherence estimates (1e-13–1e-20 s) sit 10+ orders below neural timescales; the Hagan correction to 1e-5–1e-1 s is unmeasured. The longest demonstrated microtubule quantum effect is nm-scale exciton migration.
6. **Moderate.** Endogenous fields (≤ ~4 mV/mm) do influence network timing at ≥ 0.5 mV/mm, so EM-field theories need no new physics — but equal or larger external fields (tACS ~0.5 mV/mm, TMS ~100 mV/mm) do not produce the phenomenology a field-identity theory predicts.
7. **Moderate.** Carroll's argument excludes new *local EFT* fields; it does not touch collapse modifications, non-local configuration-dependent laws, or intrinsic-nature (Russellian) views — these are the only physics-compatible loopholes.
8. **Weak.** Anaesthetic–microtubule (d = 1.9, n = 8), xenon-isotope (~45% ED50 shift) and lithium-isotope effects are each single-lab and unreplicated; large effect sizes with small n. Treat as hypotheses to test, not constraints.
9. **Weak.** Energy conservation: whole-body calorimetry closes to ~1–4% (±1–4 W); brain-specific balance is untested below ~1 W. Individual gating events cost ~23–50 kT, so any "nudge" would need ≥ several kT per channel to beat thermal noise, or coherent action across many channels.
10. **Weak.** Consciousness-collapse (IIT-CSL) has no fixed λ and no completed experiment; Φ-superposition tests on quantum hardware are proposed, not done. Substitution argument: only theories predicting a physical difference are testable.
