# 01 — Theory landscape and the evidence that discriminates

Compiled 2026-10-03. Evidence tags: **[V]** = verified, primary or repository source fetched this session; **[V2]** = verified against a fetched *secondary* source (Wikipedia, press, conference slides); **[S]** = seen only in a search-result snippet, page not fetched; **[R]** = recalled from memory, not checked.

## 1. Theories

| Theory | Core claim | Sentience IS | Distinctive prediction | Best evidence for / against |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GNWT** (Dehaene, Baars) | Content becomes conscious when "ignited" and broadcast across a fronto-parietal workspace. | Global availability of information for report, reasoning, control. Valence is just another broadcast content. | Late (~250-500 ms) nonlinear PFC ignition at onset *and offset* of a percept; all conscious content decodable from PFC. | For: masking/blink ignition signatures [R]. Against: no offset ignition and missing orientation content in PFC (Cogitate, below) [V]; P3b vanishes in no-report designs [V2]. |
| **IIT** (Tononi) | Consciousness is identical to a system's maximally irreducible cause-effect structure (Phi). | Intrinsic causal structure of the physical substrate; not computation. | Posterior "hot zone" suffices; sustained posterior synchrony for as long as a percept lasts; inactive-but-connected neurons contribute; digital computers are not conscious whatever they run. | For: inspired PCI (s.3) [V]; posterior content decoding [V]. Against: failed sustained-synchrony prediction [V]; Phi intractable; "unfolding" argument [R]. |
| **Higher-order** (Rosenthal, Lau, Brown, Fleming) | A state is conscious when suitably meta-represented (HOT, HOROR, perceptual reality monitoring, HOSS). | Being represented by the system as one's own current, real state. | Awareness can dissociate from first-order performance; PFC/metacognitive lesions alter awareness without altering discrimination. | For: blindsight; matched-performance/different-confidence studies [R]. Against: weak PFC-lesion effects on experience [R]. Adversarial tests pending (s.2). |
| **Recurrent processing** (Lamme) | Feedback within sensory cortex suffices; no frontal involvement needed. | Local recurrent sensory processing; phenomenal overflows access. | Experience without attention or report; early posterior negativity (VAN, ~200 ms) is the correlate, not P3b. | For: VAN survives no-report; registered-report meta-analysis of 117 studies finds VAN necessary, P3b not [V2]. Against: VAN seen in some "unseen-unseen" contrasts, so may not be sufficient [V2]. |
| **Predictive processing / beast machine** (Seth, Friston) | Experience is the brain's best guess; its deepest layer is interoceptive prediction regulating the body. | Affect: the felt aspect of predictive control of physiological viability. Tied to life, not intelligence. | Consciousness tracks precision-weighted interoceptive inference; substrate-dependence — computation alone is insufficient (Seth, BBS target article 2025 [S] https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/conscious-artificial-intelligence-and-biological-naturalism/C9912A5BE9D806012E3C8B3AF612E39A). | For: interoceptive manipulations shift emotion/ownership [R]. Against: a framework more than a theory; few risky predictions. INTREPID tests pending [V]. |
| **Attention schema** (Graziano) | The brain builds a simplified model of its own attention; that model's content is the claim "I am aware". | A control model's caricature of attention; a mild illusionism. | Attention without awareness is poorly controlled; TPJ damage disrupts both awareness attribution and attention control. | For: attention/awareness dissociations [R]. Against: thin direct evidence; silent on valence. |
| **Affect-first / brainstem** (Panksepp, Damasio, Solms, Merker) | Consciousness is generated subcortically (upper brainstem, PAG, hypothalamus) as affect; cortex elaborates it. | Raw valenced feeling registering homeostatic deviation. Sentience = valence, prior to perception/cognition. | Decorticate animals and hydranencephalic children stay affectively conscious; small brainstem lesions abolish all consciousness; subcortical stimulation yields the strongest affect. | For: hydranencephaly observations (Merker 2007, BBS) [S] https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/behavioral-and-brain-sciences/article/consciousness-is-more-than-wakefulness/073967D01D85F68B2C83451B99FC6E04; Panksepp stimulation work [R]. Against: brainstem may be an enabling condition rather than content-bearing; behavioural inference in non-reporters is contestable. |
| **Illusionism** (Frankish, Dennett) | Phenomenal properties do not exist; introspection misrepresents functional states as ineffable qualia. | Nothing over and above functional/reactive dispositions plus a self-model that says there is more. | The explanandum is the "meta-problem": why systems judge they have qualia. Any system with the right self-model will sincerely report ineffable experience. | For: introspective unreliability; parsimony. Against: valence seems hard to treat as mere misrepresentation. Not directly tested neurally. |
| **Russellian monism / panpsychism** (Strawson, Goff, Chalmers) | Physics describes only structure; intrinsic natures are (proto)phenomenal. | A fundamental feature of matter, combined somehow into subjects. | None empirical on its own; inherits the combination problem. IIT is its only quantitative relative. | For: closes the explanatory gap by fiat. Against: no discriminating data. |

## 2. Adversarial collaborations (Templeton World Charity Foundation, "Accelerating Research on Consciousness")

**Cogitate, Experiment 1 (IIT vs GNWT).** Published *Nature* 642:133-142, online 30 Apr 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1 [V] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12137136. n = 256 (fMRI 120, MEG 102, iEEG 34), suprathreshold stimuli of variable duration, task-relevant and irrelevant. Three preregistered contrasts [V]:

1. *Where content is decodable.* IIT: maximal in posterior cortex — passed. GNWT: PFC necessary — category decodable in PFC, but orientation was not ("limited representation of certain conscious dimensions").
2. *Maintenance over time.* IIT: content sustained for the percept's duration — sustained responses found in occipital/lateral temporal cortex, but not for orientation. GNWT: ignition at onset and offset — **offset ignition generally absent (failed)**.
3. *Connectivity.* IIT: sustained short-range posterior synchrony — **not found (failed)**. GNWT: long-range category-area-to-PFC synchrony — content-specific frontal-early-visual synchronisation found; partial.

Net: no winner; each theory lost a core prediction. A preprint of these results (2023) triggered the IIT letter below.

**IIT "pseudoscience" letter.** 124 scholars, PsyArXiv, 15 Sep 2023, DOI 10.31234/osf.io/zsr78; revised as "What makes a theory of consciousness unscientific?", *Nat Neurosci* 28:689-693 (10 Mar 2025), DOI 10.1038/s41593-025-01881-x; Tononi et al. replied in the same issue ("Consciousness or pseudo-consciousness?"). A survey of 60 researchers found 8% fully agreeing with the letter and 20% not at all [V2 — all via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_information_theory; the Nature Neuroscience page itself is paywalled and was not read]. Read: a sociological rupture, not an empirical refutation.

**Cogitate, Experiment 2** (video-game distraction, task-irrelevant faces/objects, trial-wise seen/unseen probes; MEG+EEG n = 63, fMRI n = 76). Unpublished; three papers planned for 2026-27 [V2 — ASSC 2026 slides, https://www.reed.edu/psychology/scalp/assets/conference%20files/Pitts_ASSC_talk_2026_final.pdf]. Preliminary EEG: seen-vs-unseen differences at VAN (~200-300 ms) and a fronto-central N2 (~400-600 ms); P3b absent on unprobed trials. The authors propose a two-stage model — VAN = candidate phenomenal content, fcN2 = minimal, not necessarily global, access — and flag that fcN2 would challenge RPT and IIT [V2]. Poster abstracts report PFC decoding of seen content at 250-500 ms (GNWT-consistent) and IIT's content-similarity prediction confirmed for location but not category [S — ASSC 2025 posters at reed.edu]. Treat all of this as provisional.

**Other projects** [V] https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/our-priorities/discovery/accelerating-research-consciousness/our-structured-adversarial-collaboration-projects:

- *IIT vs GNWT in animal models* (mouse/macaque, Neuropixels + optogenetics; funded Dec 2021; listed end date Sep 2026 [S] https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/projects-database/0590). No results paper found.
- *First-order vs higher-order* (RPT vs HOROR vs PRM; NYU; funded Nov 2020; grant period ended Dec 2023 [S] https://www.templetonworldcharity.org/projects-resources/project-database/0567). No results paper found.
- *INTREPID* (IIT vs active inference vs neurorepresentationalism; funded Mar 2021). Output so far is protocols plus an adversarial review, arXiv:2509.00555 (Aug 2025, revised May 2026), which states hypotheses "that will be tested" — no results [V] https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00555.
- *ETHOS* (four higher-order theories; UCL; funded Dec 2023). In progress.
- *Orch-OR vs IIT*: the group "did not identify an experiment suitable" — never ran [V].

So as of Oct 2026 only one of the collaborations has a peer-reviewed results paper.

## 3. Perturbational Complexity Index

TMS pulse, then Lempel-Ziv compressibility of the EEG response; an IIT-inspired but theory-neutral measure of integration plus differentiation. Casarotto et al. 2016 (*Ann Neurol*) [V] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5132045: benchmark of 150 subjects (102 healthy, 48 brain-injured); cutoff PCI* = 0.31 separated conscious from unconscious conditions with 100% sensitivity and specificity. Low: NREM sleep and propofol, midazolam, xenon anaesthesia. High: wake, REM, and **ketamine anaesthesia** (unresponsive but later reporting dreams). In patients: 36/38 minimally conscious above threshold (94.7%); 9/43 vegetative-state patients (21%) high-PCI, i.e. possibly covertly conscious. A small independent replication reports 11/12 MCS above threshold and 4/11 UWS high [S] https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7760168.

Significance: the best-validated measure of conscious *state*; it tracks experience rather than responsiveness. It does not distinguish theories sharply (GNWT and PP also expect complex recurrent dynamics) and says nothing about content or valence. Caveat: calibrated on reports, so the vegetative-state inference is extrapolation.

## 4. Consciousness in AI

Butlin, Long et al. 2023, arXiv:2308.08708 (19 authors): derives computational "indicator properties" from RPT, GWT, HOT, PP and AST under an assumed computational functionalism; concludes no current system is a strong candidate and no obvious technical barrier exists to building one [V] https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708. Peer-reviewed successor: "Identifying indicators of consciousness in AI systems", *Trends Cogn Sci*, DOI 10.1016/j.tics.2025.10.011 [V] https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/130322 (issue date June 2026 per search snippet [S]). The method excludes IIT, biological-naturalist and affect-first views by construction — precisely the views on which the answer flips; Seth's 2025 BBS article is the main counter-position [S].

Empirical follow-up: Lindsey (Anthropic), "Emergent introspective awareness in large language models", arXiv:2601.01828 [V] https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.01828. Injecting concept vectors into activations, models sometimes detect and name the injected concept and can distinguish their own prior intentions from prefilled text; the capacity is "highly unreliable and context-dependent". This is causally grounded self-report, relevant to higher-order and illusionist indicators, and silent on phenomenality.

## 5. Other field-shifting results, 2024-26

- **Thalamic gating.** Fang et al., *Science* (2025): intracranial recordings in 5 patients near visual threshold; intralaminar and medial thalamic nuclei show awareness-related activity earlier and stronger than PFC [V2] https://www.psypost.org/neuroscientists-identify-key-gatekeeper-of-human-consciousness. Pushes all cortico-centric theories toward thalamocortical/subcortical accounts. Tiny n.
- **No-report consolidation.** P3b is a report/task signal, not a consciousness signal; VAN is necessary [V2 — ASSC 2026 slides citing Hutchinson et al. 2026 registered-report meta-analysis; primary paper not located]. Removes GNWT's classic EEG signature while leaving a PFC contribution in some no-report fMRI/iEEG studies.
- Not found despite searching: any published result from the animal-model, RPT-vs-HOT, INTREPID or ETHOS collaborations.

## Constraints this yields

1. **Report must be separated from experience.** Late frontal P3b-type signals track task/report; any theory resting on them is undercut. *(strong)*
2. **Content is carried predominantly in posterior sensory cortex and sustained there for the percept's duration**, though not uniformly for all features. *(strong)*
3. **PFC is neither irrelevant nor a full copy of experience**: some content (category) is decodable there even when task-irrelevant; other content (orientation) is not; offset ignition is absent. Pure-posterior and pure-broadcast accounts both fail as stated. *(strong)*
4. **Sustained posterior synchrony is not the substrate**; a structural-connectivity story must explain why it is absent. *(moderate — one study, measurement-sensitivity disputed)*
5. **Conscious state tracks complexity of causal cortical interactions, not behaviour or arousal**: high in ketamine/REM, low in NREM/propofol. A theory must explain why integration and differentiation together are required. *(strong for state; says nothing about valence)*
6. **Responsiveness is an unreliable proxy**: about a fifth of vegetative-state patients show conscious-range PCI. *(moderate)*
7. **Subcortical structures are active participants**: thalamic nuclei lead PFC in awareness; brainstem integrity is necessary. Whether they *constitute* felt valence or merely enable cortex is open. *(moderate for thalamus; weak-to-moderate for affect-first claims)*
8. **At least two stages** (early posterior ~200 ms; later fronto-central ~400-600 ms) accompany seen stimuli without report. A theory must say which is phenomenal. *(weak — unpublished)*
9. **Valence is unexplained by the leading tested theories.** GNWT, IIT, RPT and HOT were tested on visual content only; no adversarial collaboration has targeted affect. A theory of sentience-as-valence is currently unconstrained by this literature and cannot borrow its support. *(strong, as a gap)*
10. **Substrate question is untested.** Functionalist indicator methods and IIT/biological naturalism give opposite verdicts on AI, and no experiment discriminates them. *(strong, as a gap)*
11. **Self-report can be partly causally grounded in internal state in systems nobody assumes are sentient** (LLM concept injection); introspective access is therefore not sufficient evidence of experience. *(moderate)*
12. **Preregistered theory predictions fail often**; treat any theory's unfalsified core as untested, not confirmed. *(strong)*
