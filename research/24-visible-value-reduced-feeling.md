# Brief 24 — Visible value, reduced feeling: what happens to planning and action?

Evidence brief for Scenario 31 (Amendment governs), evidence type (i), predictions 2, 3, 4.
Gathered 2026-10-04. **This brief does not score or adjudicate.** It reports what each
source measured and found, and which measures do or do not meet the scenario's
definitions.

## How to read this brief

**Access labels** (stated for every source):

- **FT** = full text retrieved (PubMed Central or publisher PDF) and the relevant
  methods/results passages read. I read targeted passages, not every paper cover to cover;
  numbers quoted come from those passages.
- **ABS** = abstract only.
- **SNIP** = search-engine snippet or secondary description only. Treat as unverified.

**Task labels:**

- **PLAN** = meets the scenario's operational definition of planning (devaluation or
  contingency degradation tested in extinction; model-based signature on a two-step task;
  inference on untrained combinations).
- **PLAN?** = devaluation-type design that departs from the definition in a stated way.
- **NOT-PLAN** = real-life decision making, gambling tasks, effort choice, loss aversion,
  simple choice, new-response learning. Collected as asked, but does not meet the
  definition.

**Feeling labels:**

- **GRADED** = immediate graded self-report about the options/outcomes in the task.
- **TRAIT** = questionnaire about feeling in general (SHAPS, Chapman, TEPS, TAS-20), not
  about the options in the task.
- **CLIN** = clinician rating or clinical description.
- **NONE** = feeling not measured; reduction is inferred from lesion, drug or physiology.

**Recurring limitation, stated once.** Almost no study combines all three of: (a) a PLAN
task, (b) GRADED feeling about the task's options, (c) a group in which that graded
feeling is actually reduced. Most cells below have two of the three. Where feeling was
measured by graded report in a PLAN-type task (Reber 2017; Howard 2020; Morris 2015), the
reported feeling was *normal* and the behaviour was impaired; where feeling is reduced,
the task is usually NOT-PLAN or feeling is TRAIT/CLIN/NONE.

## 8. Studies directly relating graded reported feeling to model-based / goal-directed control

Searched specifically. What exists:

- **Reber 2017** and **Howard 2020** (section 1): graded desire/pleasantness ratings and a
  devaluation choice test in the same people. Ratings shifted normally; choice did not
  follow. Feeling was *not reduced* in either.
- **Morris 2015** (section 3a, ABS): experienced value change intact in schizophrenia;
  use of it to guide action impaired.
- **Culbreth 2016**: trait SHAPS and clinician anhedonia vs two-step model-based term —
  neither significant (p=0.11; p=0.24); SHAPS related to the model-free term (p=0.03).
- **Gillan 2016**, **Patzelt 2019**, **Donegan 2025**: trait depression/apathy vs
  model-based control — null in Gillan and Donegan; mixed in Patzelt.
- **Liang 2025** (preprint): apathy–anhedonia associated with lower stated expectations
  and *better* multi-step planning.
- **Heller 2018**: anhedonia related to stress-induced loss of model-based control, not to
  baseline.

Not found: any study taking trial-wise graded feeling ratings about the options in a
two-step or extinction-devaluation task in a group with reduced feeling, with working
memory controlled. Every correlation above uses trait or clinician measures, not feeling
about the options themselves.

## 9. Where each piece of evidence touches the predictions (description only)

**Prediction 2 (visible value, not felt → planning?).** Direct tests would need reduced
graded feeling plus a PLAN task. None found. Nearest: two-step studies with trait
anhedonia/apathy (Gillan, Culbreth, Donegan, Patzelt, Liang, Heller) and schizophrenia
devaluation (Morris). In schizophrenia the laboratory reports of in-the-moment liking are
intact (Cohen & Minor; Heerey & Gold), so that group is not a reduced-graded-feeling
group by the no-rescue rule.

**Prediction 3 (proportionality between reported feeling and planning).** Sources with a
correlation between a feeling measure and a PLAN measure: Culbreth (ns), Gillan (ns for
depression, apathy), Donegan (no change in planning as symptoms improved), Patzelt
(mixed), Liang preprint (opposite sign), Heller (only under stress). All TRAIT/CLIN
measures. Working memory/IQ: related to model-based control in Culbreth; IQ covaried in
Gillan. Sources with a feeling–behaviour correlation on NOT-PLAN tasks: Treadway 2009
(Chapman and BDI-anhedonia items negative at some probability levels; SHAPS null),
Geaney 2015 (TEPS-anticipatory positive at low probability), Gold 2012/2013 (clinician
negative symptoms).

**Prediction 4 (do planner-accessible value and feeling come apart, and what happens to
action?).** Sources reporting stated knowledge of value with altered action: Bechara 1997
(3 patients, knowledge by report, feeling not rated); EVR (Saver & Damasio); SM
(Feinstein: graded fear zero, verbal knowledge of danger, approach behaviour); pain
asymbolia (Berthier, ABS); auto-activation deficit (Laplane: correct self-assessment,
blunted affect, inertia; Schmidt: normal SCR, force not scaled to incentive). Sources
reporting reduced or absent emotional response with choice intact or closer to expected
value: De Martino (expected-value and variance sensitivity normal, loss aversion absent);
Shiv 2005; Manohar 2021; Talmi 2010 (framing intact); Zhang 2017 (explicit-risk task
unimpaired in alexithymia); Heims 2004 (autonomic failure, IGT normal). Sources reporting
*normal* reported feeling with action not following it: Reber 2017; Howard 2020; Morris
2015; Heerey & Gold 2007; Xie 2018. Sources reporting behaviour/effort change with graded
ratings unchanged: Korb 2020; Eikemo 2017; Leyton 2007. Source reporting graded pleasure
reduced by drug: Buchel 2018 (choice/effort consequence not captured); Eikemo 2016
(ratings only).

## 10. Flags and caveats

1. **Reber 2017 is not an extinction test.** Rewards were still delivered at test and were
   to be eaten afterwards. n=6; 10 participants excluded for under-eating.
2. **Howard 2020 is TMS in healthy people**, a network disruption rather than a lesion; the
   effect was confined to the first probe block.
3. **Bechara 1997 counts (6 patients; 3 reaching the conceptual period)** come from
   secondary sources, not from text I read. Feeling was never rated; SCR was the proxy.
   Under the no-rescue rule SCR is not feeling.
4. **Maia & McClelland tested only healthy people.** It bears on "deciding before
   knowing", not on patients who know and do not act.
5. **Fellows & Farah** offer a non-emotional account (reversal learning) of the vmPFC IGT
   deficit; ABS only.
6. **Amygdala samples are n=1–5.** No amygdala study measured graded feeling about the
   options in the decision task itself; Feinstein's fear ratings are from separate
   real-world exposures.
7. **TAS-20 alexithymia** measures difficulty identifying and describing feelings, not
   their intensity; whether it counts as "reduced reported feeling" is for the
   adjudicator.
8. **TEPS consummatory subscale** failed its own validity check in Geaney 2015.
9. **Trait anhedonia scales disagree with each other** within one sample (Treadway 2009:
   Chapman vs SHAPS r=−0.55, opposite-direction results on effort).
10. **Korb 2020**: effort effect was specific to food and modest (about 5–7 points on a
    100-point force scale; unit as I read it, not double-checked); ratings null supported
    by Bayesian model comparison (2:1), which is weak evidence for absence.
11. **Nunez 2022** is a null for naltrexone on monetary effort with n=33; liking of the
    reward was not rated.
12. **Pain section rests on abstracts and snippets.** The lobotomy and cingulumotomy
    primary sources were not read. Congenital insensitivity removes the sensation, not
    only the feeling about it.
13. **Liang 2025 is a preprint.** Donegan 2025 was read in part.
14. **Unverified from memory and marked as such above:** Camille 2004 patient n; Wunderlich
    2012 n; Heerey & Gold working-memory link; Rogers 2004 decision result; Le Heron 2018
    result; Chelnokova 2014; Venugopalan liking ratings.
15. **Not searched or not found:** animal devaluation literature; vmPFC or amygdala lesion
    patients on the two-step task; alexithymia on two-step/devaluation; depersonalisation;
    SSRI emotional blunting (Langley 2023 surfaced in search as reduced reinforcement
    sensitivity under escitalopram, SNIP only, not followed up).
