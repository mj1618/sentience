# Research brief 28 — Which abilities are found without awareness, and is there evidence of function?

For scenario 33 (round 18), **amended version**. Covers AWARENESS (reportable experience of a
content) only — every result below manipulated or measured awareness, none manipulated
feeling. Abilities a, b, c, d, e, i, j, k, l, h; accounts N, G, U (human part), A, S, H.
Gathered 2026-10-04. **No scoring or adjudication here; evidence only.**

**Access codes** on every source:
- **FT** = full text read by me (methods/results text pulled from Europe PMC and searched).
- **FT-x** = full text summarised by an automated page extractor, not read by me line by line.
  Numbers plausible but treat as one step removed.
- **AB** = abstract only (Europe PMC record).
- **SN** = search-engine snippet / secondary summary. Unverified detail.
- **MEM** = commonly attributed detail I could not confirm on any page I could open. Do not rely on.

**Function tags** (the plan's definition):
- **F-i** awareness differs with performance/signal matched.
- **F-ii** double dissociation (blindsight-type).
- **F-iii** causal intervention (TMS, lesion, anaesthetic, instruction) removing awareness.
- **CO** co-occurrence only (ability and awareness rise and fall together; signal not matched).
- **POS-U** positive claim of "ability without awareness" (bears on whether the ability *needs* awareness, not on what awareness does).

**Post-hoc selection** = unaware participants or unseen trials picked out after the fact by the
same measure used to declare unawareness (the practice Shanks 2017 criticises).

## 0. The designs that could show function (read this first)

Only a handful of studies meet the plan's F-i/F-ii/F-iii bar. Most of the literature in
sections 1–10 is POS-U or CO.

Challenges to blindsight as a dissociation:
- Phillips 2021 *Psychol Rev* (AB): blindsight "is severely and qualitatively degraded but nonetheless conscious vision, unacknowledged due to conservative response biases"; "does not reveal any dissociation between performance and awareness".
- Ko & Lau 2012 (AB): signal-detection account — conservative criterion, low and poorly predictive confidence, detection d′ lower than forced-choice performance implies; proposed cause is a metacognitive failure.
- Persaud & Lau 2008 (AB): GY, asked directly with definitions of "qualia", denies them for stationary blind-field stimuli.
- Railo & Hurme 2021 review (AB): "blindsight may reflect preservation or partial recovery of conscious visual perception after the lesion".

**What was not found:** an intervention study that removed awareness, left first-order
discrimination matched, and then tested abilities b, c, d, or k. The matched-performance tests
that exist (Koizumi, Samaha) used confidence as the awareness proxy and found **no** gain in
inhibition, task-set preparation or working memory.

without an independent awareness measure. Trace eyeblink under surgical anaesthesia in humans:
nothing located beyond Bekinschtein's propofol control arm.

## 7. Ability j — initiating intentional action

Thin. What was found:
- Olkoniemi et al. 2023 (AB; §0.3): unseen targets rarely *initiate* a saccade (~8% of unseen TMS trials) though they steer one once launched. F-iii, single study, authors suspect weak awareness.
- van Gaal 2008–2011 (AB): masked signals modulate (slow) an already-prepared action; they "rarely" fully stop one.
- Blindsight "no spontaneous action toward blind-field objects": **unverified** (§0.2).
- Soon et al. 2008 (AB): decision outcome decodable "up to 10 s before it enters awareness". Schurger, Sitt & Dehaene 2012 (title/record only): accumulator account of the readiness potential, commonly taken to undercut the Libet-type inference. These concern awareness *of the intention*, not awareness of a stimulus content; they are POS-U-type claims about timing, not function tests.
- No study found that matched signal strength and varied awareness with action initiation as the outcome.

## 9. Ability l — flexible response to novel situations

No direct "novel situation without awareness" paradigm with matched signal was found. Indirect:
- Faivre 2014 (AB): unconscious audiovisual integration only after conscious training.
- Mudrik, Faivre & Koch 2014 (AB): unconscious integration limited to "ones that were previously acquired consciously".
- Ansorge et al. 2014 (AB): new task-set set-up needs conscious input.
- Tal & Mudrik 2024 (AB): unseen instruction flexibly initiating addition — not replicated.
- Persaud & Cowey 2008 (AB): GY cannot apply an arbitrary new rule (exclusion) to blind-field input.
- Stein & Peelen 2021 (AB): category-based attention from a verbal cue requires awareness.
- Counter-evidence: Lau & Passingham 2007 / Weibel 2013 (masked cue re-directs an *instructed* task set); Reber 2012 (unconscious inference to new word pairings; unreplicated outside lab); Alamia et al. 2016 (AB) — colour–direction associations learned without contingency awareness under "the most stringent criteria" (visible stimuli; single lab).
- From brief 25: Jurchiş et al. 2026 registered report — adaptive responding only when the predictive-value judgment was conscious; Höhmann et al. 2025 — masked stimuli elicited neither habitual nor goal-directed responses.
All CO or POS-U; none F-tagged.

## 12. Per-account pointers (evidence each side would cite; not a judgment)

**N (weak processing; N.1 lost abilities return when signal is matched)**
- For: Peters & Lau 2015 / Knotts 2018 (awareness and discrimination thresholds coincide); Koizumi 2015 and Samaha 2016 (matched d′, more awareness-proxy, no gain in e or b); Koivisto 2021 and Railo & Hurme 2021 (TMS "blindsight" is degraded conscious vision); Franco-Martínez 2026 (unconscious WM scales with sensitivity, r = .228); Phillips 2021; Zerweck 2021; Fontan 2021.
- Against: Persaud 2011 (GY: performance matched, metacognition still worse in blind field); Persaud & Cowey 2008 (exclusion errors *increase* with contrast in blind field — more signal, worse flexible use); Azzopardi & Cowey 1997; Charles 2013 (ERN step-like with visibility "regardless of masking strength"); Weidemann 2016 (identical stimulation, awareness by instruction, conditioning follows awareness); Desender 2014 (adaptation follows experienced, not actual, conflict); Peters 2017.
- Not tested: N.1 for c, d, k, l — no matched-signal study found.

**G (b, c, d, e limited without awareness; h intact)**
- For: Sackur & Dehaene 2009 (chaining fails); failed replications for c (Rabagliati; Moors & Hesselmann; Tal & Mudrik; Hesselmann 2015) and d (Moors 2016; Biderman & Mudrik 2018); Faivre 2014's training requirement; Mudrik 2014 review; Ansorge 2014; van Gaal's own "rarely" full inhibition; ERN only when seen (Charles 2013); Van den Bussche 2009 (h intact).
- Against: Franco-Martínez 2026 and Trübutschek 2017 / King 2016 (b present without reported awareness, resistant to distraction and load); van Gaal 2008–2010 and Lau & Passingham 2007 (prefrontal control networks engaged by masked signals); Carey 2012 (DB localises after 4 s delay); Koizumi 2015 / Samaha 2016 (awareness-proxy adds nothing to e, b); Chong 2014 and Henke-lab k results; Meyen 2022 / Fischer 2025 put h itself in doubt, which cuts against "h intact".

**U, human part (U.2: awareness-dependence of trace etc. is supportive, not required)**
- For: Clark & Squire 1998; Manns 2000; Clark 2001; Knight 2006 (trace SCR only for perceived CS); Bekinschtein's own framing (trace learning as a sign of awareness).
- Against / complicating: Lovibond 2011, Weidemann 2012/2013/2016 (delay conditioning *also* tracks awareness, so trace is not special); Weidemann, Broderick 2012 (trace CRs dissociate from expectancy); Arzi 2012 (trace conditioning during sleep); Mertens & Engelhard 2020; Bolaram 2020 (supports delay-without-awareness, i.e. the contrast U uses).

**A (experience needed for skeletomotor plan conflicts only)**
- For: Morsella et al. 2009 *Emotion* (AB): sustaining incompatible skeletomotor intentions changed subjective experience; "no such effects were found with conflict in a smooth muscle effector system". Morsella et al. 2009 *APP* (AB): trial-wise introspection of interference. Desender 2014 (adaptation requires experienced response conflict). Persaud & Cowey 2008 (exclusion = overriding a prepotent response fails in blind field). All CO except Desender.
- Against: masked primes create and resolve response (skeletomotor) conflict without prime awareness (Van den Bussche 2009; van Gaal 2008–2010); Koizumi 2015 (no gain in response inhibition from added confidence); awareness-dependence of abilities with no competing action (trace conditioning, WM, sentence meaning) is not predicted by A.1. Evidence on non-action conflicts (McGurk, pupil) being resolved unconsciously is theoretical in Morsella 2005 (AB); no direct test gathered.

**S (S.1: attention captured without awareness, endogenous control less stable)**
- For: Webb 2016; Wilterson 2020, 2021; Stein & Peelen 2021; Olkoniemi 2023 (initiation depends on seen targets).
- Against / limits: Kentridge 1999, 2004, 2008 and Norman 2013 (endogenous and object-based attention operate on unseen targets); no independent replication of the Graziano-lab paradigm; cue signal not matched. S's "b–f dissociate from report" finds: for — blindsight discrimination, Carey 2012, Franco-Martínez 2026; against — Peters & Lau 2015, Knotts 2018, Balsdon & Azzopardi 2015, Phillips 2021.

**H (H.1: i dissociates from first-order performance; b–e possible without awareness)**
- For: Persaud 2007, 2011 and Ko & Lau 2012 (blindsight: performance without metacognition, also when matched); Lau & Passingham 2006; Peters 2017 (TMS); Samaha 2016 and Koizumi 2015 (b, e unaffected by confidence at matched d′); Lau & Passingham 2007 and van Gaal (e without awareness); Franco-Martínez 2026 (b).
- Against: Peters & Lau 2015 / Knotts 2018 (the same lab finds *no* dissociation in normals under masking/CFS); Jannati & Di Lollo 2012 and Balsdon & Azzopardi 2015 (relative blindsight as criterion artifact); Jachs 2015 and Charles 2013 (metacognition/error detection partly *survive* low awareness — i does not go with awareness as H.1 would have it); failed replications for c and d (b–e not shown possible for chaining/novel integration); Schurger & Sher 2008 on wagering. The no-rescue rule on using report as criterion and ability at once applies to Persaud 2011.
