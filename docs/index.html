Where does feeling come from?

An ongoing investigation into sentience: reading the research, then testing each possible answer with simulations and calculations to see which survive. Each update is written to be read with no background.

  1. Update 5 · 3 October 2026A fairer test, an honest "not yet", and what a sentience field could be made ofThis round we changed how we work, to stop overstating results. We wrote down what each rival idea predicts before looking at any evidence, had one assistant gather the evidence without judging it, and had a third, independent one do the scoring. The answer that came back is modest. It is also the first one we have not had to take back.
  2. Update 4 · 3 October 2026Where in the brain is feeling? One firm result, and the wall every question hitsThis round went straight at "where does it come from": for each part of the brain, does feeling survive when that part is destroyed, and does stimulating it produce a feeling? The reviewers cut the conclusion down to one firm result. They also overturned last update's headline. Four rounds in, a pattern is clear enough to state.

Update 3 · 3 October 2026Two kinds of "chooser" are ruled out, and a list of experiments nobody has runThis round tested five new ideas about what feeling is for, and asked whether the "choosing field" from Update 2 could ever be detected. Independent reviewers again cut several of our verdicts back. What is left is smaller than we hoped but firmer: one clear elimination, one correction to a claim we made last time, and six concrete experiments that would move the question and have never been done.
  4. Update 2 · 3 October 2026A second look broke much of what we thought we had shownThis round we tested three new ideas, including your suggestion of a field that chooses instead of pushes. Then, for the first time, we handed everything to independent reviewers whose only job was to break it. They broke a lot, including claims in Update 1. This page says what fell, what stood, and what is now open.
  5. Update 1 · 3 October 2026Where does feeling come from? What the first round ruled out.We read the research, ran four small evolution experiments and one physics calculation. The main result: almost every answer of the form "feeling is an extra ingredient added to the brain" is now very hard to defend. What is left is a narrower question we can keep attacking.
