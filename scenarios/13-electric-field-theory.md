# Scenario 13 — Is the brain's own electric field the feeling?

Round 6. **Part 1 was written and committed before evidence was gathered.** Scoring is
by an independent adjudicator.

## The accounts

- **F, field-is-experience.** Conscious experience (including feeling) is the brain's
  electromagnetic field, or a pattern in it. Two versions:
  **F-loop** (McFadden): the field feeds back on neurons and so does causal work;
  **F-pattern** (Pockett): certain spatial field patterns simply are experiences.
- **N, neurons-only.** What matters is which cells fire and how they are connected. The
  field is a by-product, with at most a small local influence on spike timing.

## Part 1 — Predictions, written first

| # | Situation | F predicts | N predicts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Field feedback in living brains.** Do the brain's own fields measurably change when neurons fire? | F-loop: yes, at natural field strengths, in the intact brain. F-pattern: no commitment | Small effects on timing at most; no functional role needed |
| 2 | **A weak outside field at natural strength and rhythm** (scalp alternating current), too weak to make cells fire on its own | Experience changes in step with the applied pattern, beyond what the small change in firing explains | Effects only in proportion to changes in firing; often none |
| 3 | **Huge synchronised fields**: epileptic seizures, deep slow-wave sleep | Awkward. If field strength matters, experience should be intense. F must say the field's information content is what counts | Unconsciousness, because firing patterns are stereotyped |
| 4 | **Cells arranged so their fields cancel** (as in much of the cerebellum) | These contribute nothing to experience however much they fire | Contribution depends on wiring, not on field geometry |
| 5 | **Activity crossing a physical cut** with no nerve fibres across it | Field can carry it across | Cannot cross |
| 6 | **Split brain**: hemispheres still lie millimetres apart | Some field-carried unity should remain near the midline, unless the field falls off too fast | Division wherever fibres are cut |
| 7 | **Same firing, different field** (change how well the tissue conducts, so the same currents make a different field) | Experience changes | Experience unchanged |
| 8 | **Anaesthesia** | Loss of consciousness tracks loss of field coherence or pattern, and could occur with firing rates preserved | Tracks disrupted communication between regions |

Rows 3 and 8 are likely to be explained by both sides after the fact and are listed so
we do not later over-read them. Row 7 is the cleanest in principle and may have no data.

**What would count.** F is in trouble if row 1 shows no feedback in vivo (F-loop only),
if row 2 is cleanly null at adequate field strength, or if row 5 fails. N is in trouble
if row 2 or 7 shows experience changing with the field while firing is held fixed.
