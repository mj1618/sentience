# Scenario 9 — Four more conjectures about what feeling tracks

Evidence: `research/12-rate-ownership-medium-policy.md`. Verdicts copied from the brief.
Status: **drafted; red-team outcome at the end.**

| Conjecture | Key evidence | Verdict | Deciding experiment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rate, not level.** Good/bad tracks how fast a need is changing, not its size. | Against: thirst rises in step with how concentrated the blood is (12 trials, 167 people, r = 0.91); breathlessness tracks the level of drive; constant painful heat gets *worse* over two minutes. For: relief when pain drops slightly is outsized; momentary happiness tracks surprises more than totals. | **Refuted as the whole story; survives as one of two terms.** Level is felt. Change is felt too. | Hold blood CO₂ flat at three levels, then ramp through them at two speeds. Rate-only predicts the discomfort fades to zero on every plateau. |
| **Ownership gate.** A signal is felt only if tied to the brain's picture of its own body. | Threatening a rubber hand people feel is theirs produces fear responses. But every effect is graded; one clean test found no pain relief from the illusion; people whose sense of self dissolves (drugs, meditation) still report bliss or terror. | **Weakened.** Ownership turns feeling up or down. No evidence it is a gate. Partly unfalsifiable. | Patients who disown a limb but can still feel it: is pain in that limb unpleasant? |
| **Slow chemistry.** Feeling lives in slow, diffuse chemical signalling. | Blocking the opioid system reduces pleasure only slightly (music 6.33 → 5.78). Feelings switch on and off within seconds of deep stimulation, which is still within the chemical range. | **Weakened on opioids; otherwise untested.** Needs a stated speed limit to be testable. | With deep electrodes, time how fast the feeling follows the current. Under ~0.3 s refutes it. |
| **No ready response.** Feeling marks situations with no automatic response and fades as one is learned. | Fear of a warning signal does fade once avoidance is reliable. But pain you can control was rated *higher* in one 2025 study, and expert breath-hold divers feel normal air hunger. | **Weakened.** Holds for dread of what is coming, fails for the feeling itself. | Train an escape from heat until automatic; compare unpleasantness at identical heat with untrained controls. |

**What carries forward.** Feeling has at least two components: one that follows the
size of a bodily need and one that follows change or surprise. Any account of what
generates feeling needs both.
